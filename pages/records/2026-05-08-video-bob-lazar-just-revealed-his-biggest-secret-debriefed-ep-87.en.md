*Bob Lazar Just Revealed His BIGGEST Secret - DEBRIEFED ep. 87* is the eighty-seventh episode of the DEBRIEFED podcast, released 2026-05-08, hosted by [Chris Ramsay](/people/chris-ramsay) and [Luigi Venditelli](/people/luigi-venditelli). The episode centres on an extended interview with [Bob Lazar](/people/bob-lazar), described at the outset as an [Area 51](/places/nevada-area-51-usa) whistleblower,<sup>1</sup> and its principal disclosure is the first public naming of a security guard Lazar claims worked at [S4](/places/nevada-s4-usa).<sup>2</sup>

The name Lazar discloses is [Chuck Payne](/people/chuck-payne), whom he states he had never mentioned to anyone before this interview<sup>3</sup> — including to journalist [George Knapp](/people/george-knapp)<sup>4</sup> — and had deliberately held back as one of several details reserved for testing individuals who claim to have been involved with the programme.<sup>7</sup> Lazar describes Payne as conspicuously extroverted among an otherwise tight-lipped security contingent, greeting workers at S4 by name and standing out from the rest of the personnel.<sup>5</sup> He asserts that anyone genuinely present at S4 would have known Payne, making his name a reliable authenticity check.<sup>6</sup> Venditelli notes that he himself first heard Lazar reveal the name in a private interview more than three years before the episode's release date.<sup>8</sup>

The episode also revisits the figure of [Dennis Mariani](/people/dennis-mariani), who was originally identified by Lazar and whom Lazar now believes to have died, based on photographs of a gravestone shared with him by others.<sup>9</sup> Lazar characterises Mariani as operating on a different level from the other S4 security staff.<sup>10</sup> Corroborating context is provided by a [Janet Airlines](/organisations/janet-airlines) co-pilot who had contacted Ramsay: the pilot had flown into [Groom Lake](/places/nevada-groom-lake-usa) during the approximate period 1987 to 1991,<sup>11</sup> knew Mariani personally, confirmed his passing, and had met him on more than a dozen occasions.<sup>12</sup> After Lazar came forward through Knapp, Mariani telephoned him and arranged a meeting at the [Union Plaza Hotel](/places/nevada-las-vegas-union-plaza-hotel-usa) in [Las Vegas](/places/nevada-las-vegas-usa).<sup>13</sup> Lazar attended with witnesses including [Gene Huff](/people/gene-huff),<sup>14</sup> observed faces he recognised as S4 security personnel in the vicinity,<sup>15</sup> but found that Mariani refused to look at or acknowledge him throughout the encounter.<sup>16</sup> Lazar notes that [Chuck Payne](/people/chuck-payne) was not among those faces he saw at the hotel.<sup>15</sup>

Discussion of how Lazar came to be at S4 touches on the role of [Edward Teller](/people/edward-teller), whom Lazar credits with recommending him — referring to a young man from [Los Alamos](/places/new-mexico-los-alamos-usa) who should be given an opportunity.<sup>17</sup> Lazar also speaks at length about [Barry](/people/barry), who trained him at S4 and was assigned with him to work on the power system and [anti-gravity propulsion](/topics/anti-gravity-propulsion).<sup>19,20</sup> Their two formal directives were to duplicate the craft's propulsion system using existing materials<sup>21</sup> and to develop a means of disabling the craft's system from a distance.<sup>22</sup> The episode also addresses the three classified briefing documents Lazar was permitted to read: [Galileo (S4 propulsion briefing)](/projects/galileo-s4-propulsion-briefing), which concerned propulsion using gravity;<sup>24</sup> [Sidekick (S4 weaponisation briefing)](/projects/sidekick-s4-weaponisation-briefing), which concerned weaponisation of the craft's technology;<sup>25</sup> and [Looking Glass (S4 time briefing)](/projects/looking-glass-s4-time-briefing), which concerned [time dilation](/topics/time-dilation) and the prospect of peering forwards and backwards in time.<sup>26</sup>

The episode covers several internal control mechanisms Lazar describes. The programme, he says, inserted false and misleading information into briefings specific to each individual, so that if the information surfaced elsewhere the source of the leak could be identified.<sup>27</sup> Personnel were also given an opaque yellow fluid described to them as an immune-system booster, administered in connection with allergy testing and the presence at S4 of materials of unknown identity.<sup>28</sup> Lazar explicitly denies that this fluid caused hallucinations or impaired his clarity of mind. Regarding the broader landscape of disclosure, Lazar expresses respect for [David Grusch](/people/david-grusch) for coming forward,<sup>29</sup> and attributes the absence of further whistleblowers from the programme primarily to fear rather than to any physical suppression of witnesses.<sup>30</sup> [John Lear](/people/john-lear) is discussed briefly; Lazar recounts having told Lear directly that his more extreme claims were nonsense, and states he did not consider Lear a credible threat to those running the programme at the time. The episode also notes the involvement of [EG&G](/organisations/egg) in operating [Janet Airlines](/organisations/janet-airlines) flights and references [Project MKUltra (MKUltra)](/projects/project-mkultra-mkultra) tangentially in the context of programme conditioning claims, which Lazar rejects.
