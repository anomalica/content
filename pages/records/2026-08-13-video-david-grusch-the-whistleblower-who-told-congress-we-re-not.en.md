He said that he brought the Inspector General of the Intelligence Community (ICIG) and the [United States Congress](/organisations/united-states-congress) information about a programme recovering empirical evidence from landed or crashed UAP.<sup>1</sup> Grusch said his complaint was assessed as credible and urgent,<sup>2</sup> that it was forwarded to the congressional intelligence committees in summer 2022,<sup>3</sup> and that he later gave those committees 11 and a half hours of classified testimony.<sup>4</sup> These details make the source relevant to the institutional route by which his allegations entered formal oversight, while remaining statements attributed to Grusch rather than independently established findings.

The source then describes the substance and limits of Grusch’s account. He said he had seen what he regarded as indisputable evidence of craft on Earth that did not originate on Earth,<sup>5</sup> and that he learned about alleged retrieval and reverse engineering of recovered craft through foreign intelligence reporting, archival searches and programme documentation.<sup>6</sup> He also said the [Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA)](/organisations/defense-intelligence-agency-dia) had refused to release related classified foreign intelligence reporting to Congress.<sup>7</sup> Grusch gave an estimated holding of more than 25 but fewer than 100 craft of non-human origin,<sup>8</sup> and listed disc-shaped,<sup>9</sup> sunfish- or boot-heel-shaped,<sup>10</sup> boomerang-shaped,<sup>11</sup> crescent-moon-shaped,<sup>12</sup> Tic Tac-shaped<sup>13</sup> and egg-shaped vehicles.<sup>14</sup> He said their origin was difficult to determine because their point in space or time was unknown,<sup>15</sup> while identifying propulsion as a motive for the reverse engineering of recovered craft.<sup>16</sup> Grusch cautioned against treating the [extraterrestrial hypothesis](/topics/extraterrestrial-hypothesis) as the complete explanation, mentioning ultraterrestrial hypothesis and a possible shadow biome as alternatives.<sup>17</sup><sup>18</sup><sup>19</sup> He also acknowledged that he lacked full exposure to government holdings and analysis and did not have all the answers.<sup>20</sup> The source therefore matters both for the allegations it records and for its explicit presentation of their limits.
