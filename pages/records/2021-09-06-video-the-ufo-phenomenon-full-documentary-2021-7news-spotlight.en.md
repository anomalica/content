*The UFO Phenomenon | Full Documentary 2021 | 7NEWS Spotlight* is a 2021 documentary examining reported [Unidentified Flying Object (UFO)](/topics/unidentified-flying-object-ufo) and [Unidentified Aerial Phenomena (UAP)](/topics/unidentified-aerial-phenomena-uap) encounters. It combines interviews, historical cases, military accounts and claims about government investigations, making it a record of both reported observations and the interpretations offered by participants.

## Scope and participants

The documentary presents [Ross Coulthart](/people/ross-coulthart)'s year-long investigation into UAP, which he documented in a book and on camera.<sup>1</sup> It includes commentary from former officials, witnesses, researchers and military personnel. According to the documentary, Christopher Mellon was a deputy assistant secretary in the [United States Department of Defense (DoD)](/organisations/united-states-department-of-defense-dod) during the Clinton and Bush administrations. Mellon said that some observed UAP might be intelligently controlled, while also identifying Russian or Chinese technology as a possible explanation and noting the reported performance as difficult to attribute to terrestrial rivals.

The programme discusses the DoD's reported admission in April 2020 that videos recorded by fighter pilots showed real unidentified objects.<sup>2</sup> It also describes [Luis Elizondo](/people/luis-elizondo) as a former US government intelligence officer and says that he was put in charge of the [Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program (AATIP)](/projects/advanced-aerospace-threat-identification-program-aatip).<sup>3</sup> Elizondo said that AATIP determined that a real phenomenon was present in the sky, while its nature, method of operation, intent and full capabilities remained unknown.<sup>4</sup>

The documentary further reports that [The New York Times](/organisations/the-new-york-times) revealed a Pentagon unit investigating UFOs in December 2017, accompanied by videos.<sup>5</sup> [Leslie Kean](/people/leslie-kean) said that a secret programme studying unidentified objects had operated within the Pentagon for ten years from 2007.<sup>6</sup> Elizondo described the reported technology as involving instantaneous acceleration, hypersonic velocity, low observability, trans-medium travel and [anti-gravity propulsion](/topics/anti-gravity-propulsion). These descriptions are presented as testimony and interpretation rather than as an independently established technical assessment.

## 2004 USS Nimitz encounter

A substantial section concerns the [2004 USS Nimitz UAP encounter](/events/2004-uss-nimitz-uap-encounter). According to the documentary, on 2004-11-14 the [USS Nimitz](/objects/uss-nimitz-cvn-68) was conducting drills off the Southern Californian coast before deployment to the Persian Gulf.<sup>7</sup> The [USS Princeton](/objects/uss-princeton-cg-59) was another ship involved in the exercise, with [Kevin Day](/people/kevin-day) manning its radar.<sup>8</sup>

Day said that he observed a formation of unidentified flying objects rather than the simulated adversary aircraft he expected.<sup>9</sup> [David Fravor](/people/david-fravor) was airborne and was ordered to intercept and identify the objects.<sup>10</sup> The documentary reports that, as Fravor approached, the object described as a tic-tac appeared to recognise and respond to his movements.<sup>11</sup> It also says that the object manoeuvred evasively to maintain its distance from his F/A-18 Super Hornet.

Day reportedly tracked the object during the intercept and estimated that it descended from approximately 8,500 metres to the ocean surface in 0.78 seconds.<sup>12</sup> Elizondo said that it then disappeared instantaneously and was reported five seconds later at the aircraft's cap point. The cap point was described as an aerial rendezvous location approximately 97 kilometres away, known only to a limited number of Navy personnel and pilots.<sup>13</sup> The documentary states that the object was already waiting there when the pilots arrived, although the location had been known only inside the [USS Nimitz](/objects/uss-nimitz-cvn-68) briefing room.<sup>14</sup>

Elizondo described the object as having no visible propulsion, obvious signs of lift, wings, rudders, control surfaces, ailerons, elevators, cockpit or rivets. The documentary also attributes to an unnamed retired US Navy commander the view that the observed accelerations would be sufficient to destroy any aircraft designed or built at the time, and that no visible propulsion system was present. These descriptions form part of the documentary's presentation of the encounter and are not accompanied here by an independent technical determination.

## Exmouth and the Harold Holt sighting

The documentary follows Coulthart to Australia, Western Australia, Exmouth, where he investigated reported unusual aerial activity.<sup>15</sup> Annie recalled that residents had seen a UFO and that the US military wanted to know what they had seen and wanted the matter kept quiet.<sup>16</sup> The area is presented in relation to the Australia, Western Australia, Harold Holt Naval Communications Station, described as a secret military base that communicated with US nuclear submarines during the Cold War.<sup>17</sup>

The account centres on the 1991 Harold Holt Naval Communications Station UFO sighting. Annie said that she saw a diamond-shaped craft hovering directly above the vehicle in which she was travelling.<sup>18</sup> She estimated its length as two to three cars, reported lights on its underside, and said that it made no sound before shooting straight upwards faster than she could visually track.<sup>19</sup> The craft was also described as deep grey, with an elongated pointed front, a cut-away rear and lighting along its spine and wings. Annie said that its upper surface had no windows and appeared to be solid metal.

According to the documentary, Annie was travelling with two officers who were then known as Protective Service officers and later became part of the [Australian Federal Police](/organisations/australian-federal-police). Coulthart said that Annie and the officers were cross-examined and interrogated by Americans at the base in the days following the sighting.<sup>20</sup> Annie said that they were instructed to tell others that they had seen a weather balloon.<sup>21</sup> Coulthart characterised the questioning as a secret American investigation intended to silence her, while Annie attributed it to the shock of the Americans who questioned her. These are the views of the named speakers.

A possible conventional explanation discussed in the documentary is the Hopeless Diamond, described as a stealth-aircraft design shown to Annie. Annie said that it resembled what she had seen, except that the top of the displayed design had more three-dimensional cuts and its tail was truncated. Coulthart suggested that, if the 1991 craft had been human-built, it might indicate that Americans were testing advanced technology over Exmouth.<sup>22</sup>

The documentary also states that Coulthart's crew were stopped from filming near the station by Australia's Defence Department and were asked to leave the area by an official. The crew subsequently had to film from a hill approximately 10 kilometres away. Coulthart said that Australia continued transmitting on behalf of the Americans from the station and that some activities remained secret. He also said that sighting reports were held in Australia's National Archives and that the Australian government possessed thousands of UFO reports, many of which had once been classified secret or top secret.<sup>23</sup>

## Nuclear weapons and military installations

The source presents a proposed relationship between UFO reports and nuclear facilities. It states that there appeared to be a strong correlation between nuclear weapons, nuclear storage sites and UFO sightings. Robert Salas, described as a retired [United States Air Force](/organisations/united-states-air-force-usaf) weapons operator, said that UFOs were focused on nuclear weapons and that interference with them might be intended as a message to eliminate nuclear weapons.

Salas's account of the 1967 Malmstrom Air Force Base UFO incident is also included. The documentary says that he was in an underground missile facility on 1967-03-24 when a guard reported a large orange-red pulsating light hovering above the front gate.<sup>24</sup> Salas said that alarms then went off across the facility, indicating that missiles were going offline.<sup>25</sup> He believes that something shut down the missile system and that the objects knew how the systems worked and could interfere with them. He further interprets the incident as evidence of a non-human intelligence with technology vastly superior to human technology, and believes that its source was off Earth.

## Australian historical cases

The documentary discusses the 1966 Westall High School UFO sighting at Westall High School in [Australia, Victoria, Melbourne](/places/victoria-melbourne-australia). Greenwood said that he was teaching a year nine science class when a pupil reported a flying saucer. Greenwood then saw a grey, almost cylindrical or cigar-shaped object over the far side of the school oval, moving with some precision.<sup>26</sup> He said that students ran across the oval after the object moved behind pine trees. The documentary reports that more than 200 people witnessed the sighting.<sup>27</sup>

Other testimony in the programme describes an object on the ground surrounded by heat and purple lights before it rose slowly to approximately three or four metres and flew straight upwards. Air Force and Army personnel reportedly arrived within 40 minutes, and returning witnesses found a circular area of trampled grass with guards and people using equipment.

Greenwood said that two government officials later visited his home and told him that he had seen nothing. He further said that they threatened to report him as drunk on duty and have him lose his job if he continued to say that he had seen a UFO. The documentary states that, twelve months later, officials from the [Office of the President of the United States](/organisations/office-of-the-president-of-the-united-states) approached him about the incident. According to Greenwood, those officials said that the President had authorised a board of inquiry, and James McDonald was sent to interview him.

McDonald is described as having worked officially for the [United States Navy](/organisations/united-states-navy-usn) while investigating Australian UFO reports in his spare time. The documentary says that he examined approximately 50 to 60 cases in Australia and New Zealand and found them essentially similar to reports from the United States, including accounts of discs and cigar-shaped objects hovering at low elevation over urban areas or over and behind cars.

## Roswell and alleged recovered technology

The documentary presents the 1948 Roswell UFO incident through David Mahler, [Scott Ramsey](/people/scott-ramsey) and [Suzanne Ramsey](/people/suzanne-ramsey). An anonymous source claiming to be a senior US defence or intelligence official told Coulthart that the United States had recovered alien extraterrestrial technology, including spacecraft, and that one recovered craft came from near [USA, New Mexico, Roswell](/places/new-mexico-roswell-usa).<sup>28</sup> The attribution to an anonymous source is retained in the documentary's account.

According to Scott Ramsey, oil workers discovered a lenticular craft on 1948-03-25 after being called out because of a brush fire. He estimated the object as approximately 5.5 metres tall and just under 30 metres across.<sup>29</sup> Suzanne Ramsey said that research by her and Scott indicated that 16 dead alien bodies were found aboard the recovered craft. She described the bodies as between approximately 1.1 and 1.2 metres tall, childlike in appearance, with perfect teeth, and wearing charred powder-blue jumpsuits without visible rank markings.

The documentary also includes Krista, who is described as the granddaughter of a man reportedly said to have seen the craft strike another mesa before crashing. Family folklore relayed by Krista holds that the craft was wobbling and out of control before the impact and continued moving afterwards. Coulthart used a drone to obtain photographs and video of a mark on a rock at the mesa believed to have been struck by the craft.

Ramsey said that the recovered craft was taken to [Los Alamos National Laboratory](/organisations/los-alamos-national-laboratory), while Coulthart reported witnesses claiming to have worked on a recovered alien spacecraft and saying that the US government was attempting to reverse-engineer its technology. These statements are presented as attributed claims rather than established findings.

## Other reports

The documentary includes the 1973 Exmouth drive-in triangle sighting, reported by Adrian Arnold, who was 12 years old at the time. Arnold said that he saw three lights moving rapidly in one direction, pausing and then moving in another direction. He described them as forming a triangle that sometimes distorted and changed shape before returning to a flat triangle. Arnold believed that the movement could not have been produced by a plane or helicopter.

The 2015 Exmouth light sighting is attributed to Nikolai Gordovich, who said that he was alone on the road early one morning when he saw a pure, bright white light on top of a hill. He thought it might have been connected with the military and said that he had absolute clarity of his memory of the event.

The documentary also recounts the Narre Warren UFO sighting through Grant Denyer. Coulthart said that Denyer contacted him about objects hovering over Narre Warren in south-east Melbourne when Denyer was about 19. Denyer said that, while driving at night at about 9pm, he saw several very bright lights hovering low in the sky. He estimated about five lights in two groups; one reportedly left its group, accelerated to supersonic speed, travelled to the other group and stopped instantly.<sup>30</sup> Denyer believes that what he saw was some form of UFO.

## Cattle mutilation reports

A final section addresses the cattle mutilation phenomenon. [Mick Cook](/people/mick-cook) and [Judy Cook](/people/judy-cook) are described as operating a 14,000-hectare cattle station in an isolated part of North Queensland. Mick said that he found a cow whose ear and part of its jowl had been surgically removed without blood on the hide, and that he saw no struggle marks at the site.<sup>31</sup> Judy considered the lack of blood and struggle marks unexplained. The documentary reports that 15 cattle subsequently died in similar circumstances.<sup>32</sup>

The programme says that, during research for a book, the [Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI)](/organisations/federal-bureau-of-investigation-fbi) told an interviewer that there were 10,000 unsolved cattle-mutilation cases in the United States.<sup>33</sup> It further states that the issue had become significant enough for congressmen to demand federal investigations, while federal law enforcement had not been able to explain the cases. Judy Cook considers the mutilations on the property to be alien in origin; the documentary presents that conclusion as her opinion rather than as a demonstrated cause.
