Horn describes being interrogated over roughly five to six months by a retired [United States Air Force Office of Special Investigations (AFOSI)](/organisations/united-states-air-force-office-of-special-investigations-afosi) and [Department of Defense (DoD)](/organisations/department-of-defense-dod) case supervisor and investigator,<sup>20</sup> who concluded the case is 100% ironclad authentic.<sup>21</sup> Similarly, [Joe Tisk](/people/joe-tisk) assessed the case as 100% authentic, noting not just one authentic photo but rolls of them.<sup>27</sup> Ramsay relays that [Bob Lazar](/people/bob-lazar) identified the [Billy Meier beam ship (sports model craft)](/objects/billy-meier-beam-ship-sports-model-craft) as a one-for-one identical match to craft he was familiar with,<sup>5</sup> though both Ramsay and Lazar consider the later Meier material questionable.<sup>6</sup> Horn notes that investigators including [Wendell Stevens](/people/wendell-stevens) had a model of one of the craft made at [Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer (MGM)](/organisations/metro-goldwyn-mayer-mgm) studios in Hollywood and conducted comparative photographic tests at Meier's specified locations, with computer analysis clearly showing the model lacked the energy fields visible in Meier's photographs. Meier's ex-wife and 16 others associated with the case underwent voice-stress lie-detector testing and all passed as authentic.<sup>32</sup> In 1964 in India, Meier was interviewed by a journalist in a prominent newspaper who viewed an album containing 80 UFO photographs,<sup>28</sup> with Meier having used an early [Kodak box camera](/objects/kodak-box-camera) for those images.<sup>29</sup> In the 1990s a woman referred to as [Chang](/people/chang), who had served as Cambodia's ambassador or representative to the [United Nations (UN)](/organisations/united-nations-un), came forward as an eyewitness and stated there were a hundred eyewitnesses at the India location.<sup>30</sup>

A significant section addresses the prophetic documents attributed to Meier and his contacts. [Asket](/people/asket), identified as Meier's second extraterrestrial contact,<sup>83</sup> is said to have given Meier prophecies as early as 1953, including that two brothers running for president in America would be assassinated, and naming that [John Fitzgerald Kennedy](/people/john-fitzgerald-kennedy) would be killed in [USA, Texas, Dallas](/places/texas-dallas-usa) on a specified day.<sup>40</sup> The [Henoch Prophecies](/documents/henoch-prophecies) were given to Meier in 1987 and contain about 300 specific predictions,<sup>36</sup> with the first English version appearing around 2002; they describe the destruction of the [World Trade Center](/objects/world-trade-center),<sup>37</sup> which occurred during the [September 11 attacks](/events/september-11-attacks). In 1989 a conversation with [Quetzal](/people/quetzal) is said to have specified that terrorists would fly aeroplanes into the World Trade Center buildings and that about 3,000 people would be killed.<sup>38</sup> The Henoch document also mentions Russian troops moving to [Arkhangelsk Russia](/places/arkhangelsk-russia) in preparation for attacks against Scandinavia,<sup>39</sup> a passage Horn connects to Norway, Sweden and Finland joining [North Atlantic Treaty Organisation (NATO)](/organisations/north-atlantic-treaty-organisation-nato) and to Russia establishing military bases in Arkhangelsk in 2011. In the [Billy Meier 1982 Swiss radio interview](/documents/billy-meier-1982-swiss-radio-interview), Meier stated a third world war would involve conflict between the United Kingdom and Russia, with other countries including Iran, [Jerusalem Israel](/places/jerusalem-israel), [Baghdad Iraq](/places/baghdad-iraq) and the [Falkland Islands](/places/falkland-islands). Horn also cites a 1958 statement by Meier warning that first America and then the European Union would biochip every person on the planet to control their movement to the metre.<sup>50</sup>

The episode discusses the [Giza Intelligences (Befath)](/organisations/giza-intelligences-befath) and the [Billy Meier and Asket Giza pyramid visit](/events/billy-meier-and-asket-giza-pyramid-visit) at length. Horn states the [Pleiaren](/organisations/pleiaren) and the Giza Intelligences both originate from the same Lyrian source group but diverged,<sup>33</sup> with the Befath using a telepathic technology called tele-nautic to influence around 745 political and religious leaders<sup>34</sup> and planning a false second coming through implanted persons.<sup>34</sup> Asket brought Meier to the [Egypt, Giza, Great Pyramid of Giza](/places/giza-great-pyramid-of-giza-egypt), where the two used devices rendering them invisible and communicated strictly by [telepathy](/topics/telepathy).<sup>41</sup> The chamber they reached is described as about one to two kilometres below the pyramid and containing large and small craft;<sup>42</sup> there Meier saw apparent crucifixion relics including the [Spear of Destiny](/objects/spear-of-destiny) and [Crown of Thorns](/objects/crown-of-thorns), which Asket said were fabricated by the Befath and engineered to carbon-date to the crucifixion period.<sup>43</sup> The [Befath sonic attack on Billy Meier's centre](/events/befath-sonic-attack-on-billy-meiers-centre) is also described: a robotically controlled Befath craft attempted to synchronise a sonic beam with cannon fire in the valley to destroy the centre, but the Pleiaren, who had screened a craft above the centre, took hold of the Befath vessel and subsequently removed the Befath from beneath the pyramid, leaving no trace of their headquarters.
