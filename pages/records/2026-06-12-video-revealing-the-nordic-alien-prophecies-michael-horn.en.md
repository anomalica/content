"Revealing The Nordic Alien Prophecies" is episode 92 of the podcast DEBRIEFED (podcast), recorded on 2026-06-12, in which host Chris Ramsay interviews researcher [Michael Horn](/people/michael-horn) about the [Billy Meier](/people/billy-meier) Billy Meier UFO contact case. Horn says that Meier claims to have been tutored from around age five to sixteen by a being named Sfath, said at the time to be about 900 years old.<sup>1</sup> Horn describes Meier as an 89-year-old man who has met since childhood with beings said to originate from another star system,<sup>2</sup> and says Meier is claimed to have taken approximately 1,200 authenticated UFO photographs.<sup>3</sup> Ramsay characterises Meier alongside [Bob Lazar](/people/bob-lazar) as one of the two most contentious names in ufology,<sup>4</sup> and says there are over a thousand Billy Meier contact reports.<sup>5</sup> He also notes that the "I Want to Believe" poster from The X-Files (TV series) is a cropped version of a Meier photograph.<sup>6</sup>

Much of the discussion concerns the photographic and physical evidence in the case. Ramsay says Lazar identified the Billy Meier beam ship (sports model craft) as a one-for-one match to craft he was familiar with,<sup>7</sup> though he adds that he and Lazar both regard the later Meier material as questionable.<sup>8</sup> Ramsay says the iconic craft photograph was taken in Switzerland around 1975.<sup>9</sup> Discussing the Wedding cake UFO photographs, Ramsay says he identified forced-perspective inconsistencies in them,<sup>10</sup> and that sceptics have said the nighttime images were made using a model painted gold and photographed against a black curtain.<sup>11</sup> Ramsay separately concluded that one close-up nighttime wedding cake car photograph, being out of focus and depicting an object the size of a toy car, was a hoax.<sup>12</sup> Horn describes counter-tests: investigators including Wendell Stevens had a model craft built at the Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer (MGM) studios and photographed under the same conditions Meier used,<sup>13</sup> with computer analysis of the resulting images showing no energy fields around the model object.<sup>14</sup> Horn says Marcel Vogel, an IBM scientist, said he could not replicate the Billy Meier metal samples with any technology available to him and described them as a cold fusion product,<sup>15</sup> having detected fine laser cuttings and an unusual elemental composition under high magnification.<sup>16</sup> Horn also says [Gordon Cooper](/people/gordon-cooper) examined the photographs and judged them genuine.<sup>17</sup> Horn recounts that in 1964, while Meier was in India, he was interviewed by a newspaper that photographed an album of 80 UFO photographs,<sup>18</sup> taken with an early Kodak box camera.<sup>19</sup> He names Asket as Meier's second alleged contact person,<sup>20</sup> and says he personally spoke to 14 eyewitnesses and saw bullet holes and investigative reports connected to the case.<sup>21</sup> Horn separately says a retired United States Air Force Office of Special Investigations (AFOSI) and Department of Defense investigator questioned him over roughly five to six months<sup>22</sup> before concluding the case was "100% ironclad authentic."<sup>23</sup>

A substantial portion of the episode covers claimed prophecies attributed to Meier's contacts. Horn says that as early as 1953, Asket told Meier that [John Fitzgerald Kennedy](/people/john-fitzgerald-kennedy) would be assassinated in Dallas, Texas.<sup>24</sup> In 1987 Meier is said to have received the Henoch Prophecies, containing about 300 specific predictions,<sup>25</sup> which Horn says describe the destruction of the World Trade Center;<sup>26</sup> he separately says that in 1989 Meier was told terrorists would fly aeroplanes into the World Trade Center and kill about 3,000 people.<sup>27</sup> Horn says the 1987 Henoch document mentions Russian troops moving to Arkhangelsk Russia ahead of attacks on Scandinavia,<sup>28</sup> and connects this to Norway, Sweden and Finland later joining the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation (NATO)<sup>29</sup> and to Russia establishing military bases in Arkhangelsk in 2011.<sup>30</sup> In the 1982 Swiss radio interview, Horn says Meier described a third world war as a certainty,<sup>31</sup> involving the United Kingdom, Russia, Iran, Israel, Iraq and the Falkland Islands.<sup>32</sup> Horn also says that as early as 1946 and 1948, Meier's teacher described a future incompetent, self-aggrandising US dictator figure.<sup>33</sup> On the Discovery of the Talmud of Jmmanuel scrolls, Horn says that around 1963-1964 Meier and Isa Rashid dug up scrolls at a tomb near Jerusalem Israel,<sup>34</sup> and that Isa Rashid and his family were later machine-gunned to death in Baghdad Iraq around 1974, reportedly by the Israeli Secret Service (Mossad).<sup>35</sup> Per the Talmud of Jmmanuel (TJ), Horn says the man crucified and known as Jesus Christ was in fact named Jmmanuel,<sup>36</sup> who is said to have foretold that a prophet named Muhammad would arise in 500 years.<sup>37</sup> Horn places Meier as the seventh and final figure in a prophetic lineage including Enoch, Isaiah, Jeremiah, Jmmanuel and Muhammad.<sup>38</sup>

The conversation also addresses the Billy Meier and Asket Giza pyramid visit, in which Horn says Meier and Asket entered an underground chamber beneath the Egypt, Giza, Great Pyramid of Giza.<sup>39</sup> There, Horn says Meier saw relics associated with the crucifixion that Asket said had been fabricated by the Giza Intelligences (Befath).<sup>40</sup> Horn describes the Giza Intelligences as a splinter group sharing origins with the Pleiaren, tracing back to the constellation Lyra,<sup>41</sup> and says the group held influence over around 745 political and religious leaders.<sup>42</sup> He recounts the Befath sonic attack on Billy Meier's centre in the Zurich Highlands Switzerland, which he dates to around 1975-1978,<sup>43</sup> and says the attack was met by the Pleiaren eliminating the Befath ship and removing the group from beneath the pyramid.<sup>44</sup> Horn attributes to Meier an account of losing his arm and having his teeth smashed, followed by 30 days of delirium after amputation without anaesthetic.<sup>45</sup> He also says Meier declined a Pleiaren offer to regrow the arm, citing a risk of military kidnapping and dissection.<sup>46</sup>

Later in the episode, Horn discusses the creation energy teaching (Geisteslehre), under which, he says, there are no deities and no religion is founded on a true creator god.<sup>47</sup> He attributes to Meier the view that the meaning of life is the evolution of consciousness,<sup>48</sup> and describes the teaching's position on [reincarnation](/topics/reincarnation) as holding that it is real but that no person retains memory of past lives.<sup>49</sup> The pair also discuss [remote viewing](/topics/remote-viewing-rv) and [Ed Dames](/people/ed-dames): Horn says he helped create Dames's first remote viewing home study course over more than a year,<sup>50</sup> but that he personally saw nothing accurate from Dames's remote viewing over roughly a year working with him.<sup>51</sup> Ramsay notes that Dames died shortly before this episode was recorded.<sup>52</sup> Finally, Horn connects a 1976 Meier prophecy describing seven-foot-tall giants emerging from hiding in Peru<sup>53</sup> to the 2023 Peru seven-foot giants reports, in which such figures were reported abducting and harming people in around August 2023,<sup>54</sup> saying the event's location and the described size of the figures matched the prophecy roughly 47 years after it was published.<sup>55</sup> Horn closes by saying he does not "believe" in Meier or UFOs, characterising his position instead as based on his own direct experience.<sup>56</sup>
