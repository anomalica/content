FOIA Response 18-F-0324: AATIP and Luis Elizondo Documents is a package of US government records released by the [Department of Defense (DoD)](/organisations/department-of-defense-dod) in response to a Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) request filed by [Greenewald Jr., John](/people/john-greenewald-jr) on 2017-12-17,<sup>1</sup> received by the DoD the following day and assigned case number 18-F-0324.<sup>2</sup> The [Office of Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence (OUSD(I))](/organisations/office-of-under-secretary-of-defense-for-intelligence-ousdi) conducted a search and located eight pages responsive to the request,<sup>3</sup> portions of which were withheld under 5 U.S.C. § 552(b)(6) to protect personal privacy.<sup>4</sup> [Brian C. Kreitlow](/people/brian-c-kreitlow) served as Initial Denial Authority,<sup>5</sup> and [Stephanie L. Carr](/people/stephanie-l-carr), Chief of the DoD Freedom of Information Division, signed the response on 2022-08-17.<sup>6</sup> Appeals could be directed to [Joo Chung](/people/joo-chung), Assistant to the Secretary of Defense for Privacy, Civil Liberties, and Transparency, at the [Office of the Secretary of Defense](/organisations/office-of-the-secretary-of-defense) in [USA, Virginia, Alexandria](/places/virginia-alexandria-usa),<sup>7</sup> with mediation available through the [Office of Government Information Services (OGIS)](/organisations/office-of-government-information-services-ogis) at the [National Archives](/organisations/national-archives) in [USA, Maryland, College Park](/places/maryland-college-park-usa).<sup>8</sup>

The substantive records within the package centre on a memorandum authored by [Garry Reid](/people/garry-reid), identified as Director for Defense Intelligence within OUSD(I),<sup>9</sup> summarising his account of events surrounding [Luis Elizondo](/people/luis-elizondo) and the [Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program (AATIP)](/organisations/advanced-aerospace-threat-identification-program-aatip). Reid stated that Elizondo was assigned to OUSD(I) from 2008-09-28 to 2017-10-04,<sup>10</sup> serving first as an action officer in Partner Engagement<sup>11</sup> and subsequently as a branch chief managing classified information in support of the [Office of Military Commissions](/organisations/office-of-military-commissions).<sup>12</sup> Reid stated that, to the best of his knowledge, Elizondo had no job responsibilities related to AATIP,<sup>13</sup> and that AATIP had been terminated in 2012.<sup>14</sup> The package also contains the [Garry Reid SecDef Response Memo](/documents/garry-reid-secdef-response-memo), addressed to the [Office of the Secretary of Defense](/organisations/office-of-the-secretary-of-defense) and structured around six questions concerning Elizondo's actions and AATIP.<sup>15</sup>

The released documents include two letters associated with [Elizondo Pentagon Resignation 2017](/events/elizondo-pentagon-resignation-2017). The [Elizondo Resignation Letter](/documents/elizondo-resignation-letter), dated 2017-10-03, stated his resignation was effective 2017-10-04 from his role as Director, National Programs Special Management Staff,<sup>16</sup> and requested that he receive a [Deferred Retirement Policy DoD](/concepts/deferred-retirement-policy-dod).<sup>17</sup> Reid's account records that Elizondo provided no stated reason for his departure in that initial letter<sup>18</sup> and was cleared out of the OUSD(I) office on 2017-10-04.<sup>19</sup> On or about that same date, a second letter — the [Elizondo Second Resignation Letter (Memorandum for Record)](/documents/elizondo-second-resignation-letter-memorandum-for-record) — was hand-delivered to the OUSD(I) Chief of Staff office by someone other than Elizondo himself.<sup>20</sup> Reid noted the document was formatted as a Memorandum for Record but used the salutation "Mr. Secretary,"<sup>21</sup> and that due to uncertain provenance OUSD(I) retained a copy but did not forward it to the Secretary of Defense's office.<sup>22</sup> The second letter cited concerns over anomalous aerospace threats as the basis for resignation<sup>23</sup> and, as recorded within the released documents, stated that Elizondo had served over 22 years with America's military,<sup>24</sup> that "overwhelming evidence at both the unclassified and classified levels" of anomalous aerospace threats existed,<sup>25</sup> that the phenomena could constitute a tactical threat to pilots, sailors, and soldiers and perhaps an existential threat to national security,<sup>26</sup> and that there appeared to be a direct correlation between the phenomena and US nuclear and military capabilities.<sup>27</sup>

Reid's memorandum describes several events following Elizondo's departure. In a phone call on 2017-10-05, Elizondo told Reid he had been secretly tasked by the Secretary of Defense as programme manager for investigating UFOs and other aerial threats, and that nobody in OUSD(I) was cleared for the alleged programme.<sup>28</sup> Reid discussed these claims with senior officials who would likely have known of such an arrangement but was unable to substantiate them,<sup>29</sup> and he and two senior officials with knowledge of special programmes collectively assessed Elizondo's claims as not credible.<sup>30</sup> On 2017-10-12, Reid learned of a video featuring Elizondo posted on [YouTube](/organisations/youtube) by [To the Stars Academy of Arts and Science](/organisations/to-the-stars-academy-of-arts-and-science), in which Elizondo stated he had run "a sensitive Aerospace Threat Identification Program focusing on unidentified aerial technologies" for nearly the last decade.<sup>31</sup> Following discovery of the video, Reid requested an informal review of Elizondo's conduct by the [Air Force Office of Special Investigations (AFOSI)](/organisations/air-force-office-of-special-investigations-afosi).<sup>32</sup> In late 2017-11, AFOSI advised Reid that absent more tangible indicators of misconduct they could not open a case,<sup>33</sup> and their informal assessment did not find any wrongdoing.<sup>34</sup>

The timeline recorded in the package extends through 2017-12. On 2017-12-07, the DoD was contacted by [The New York Times](/organisations/the-new-york-times) seeking information about AATIP.<sup>35</sup> On 2017-12-15, Reid notified the OUSD(I) Security Officer that Elizondo may have misused government systems and may intend to release US government footage or information obtained during his employment.<sup>36</sup> On 2017-12-18, OUSD(I) located a classified email generated by Elizondo in 2017-08, associated with the [Elizondo Classified Email Declassification Request 2017-08](/events/elizondo-classified-email-declassification-request-2017-08), in which he had requested assistance from a Navy civilian employee to declassify one or more videos.<sup>37</sup> The video attached to that email was identified as the same footage broadcast on national news on 2017-12-17.<sup>38</sup> The email chain was incomplete, leaving OUSD(I) unable to determine whether the video was properly declassified or how it had reached the media;<sup>39</sup> the Navy civilian employee had informed OUSD(I) that he told Elizondo he was not the declassification release authority and took no further action.<sup>40</sup> On 2017-12-21, Reid met with [AFOSI](/organisations/air-force-office-of-special-investigations-afosi) agents to initiate a formal inquiry into Elizondo's possible mishandling of classified materials, corresponding to the [Elizondo AFOSI Formal Inquiry 2017-12](/events/elizondo-afosi-formal-inquiry-2017-12).<sup>41</sup> The FOIA request itself was submitted by [Greenewald Jr., John](/people/john-greenewald-jr) — associated with [The Black Vault](/organisations/the-black-vault) — two days earlier, on 2017-12-17.<sup>1</sup> The action officer assigned to the case was [Daniel Mullin](/people/daniel-mullin), and the overarching FOIA matter is catalogued as [FOIA 18-F-0324: AATIP and Elizondo Documents](/matters/foia-18-f-0324-aatip-and-elizondo-documents). Elizondo completed all administrative and security out-processing, including formal debriefs for all [Special Access Programs (concept)](/concepts/special-access-programs-concept), Sensitive Compartmented Information, and compartmented programmes, within approximately 24 hours of his 2017-10-03 notification,<sup>42</sup> a detail Reid included in his response to the Secretary of Defense when addressing the circumstances of the [Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program (AAWSAP) Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program (AATIP) Cancellation 2012](/events/advanced-aerospace-weapon-system-applications-program-aawsap-advanced-aerospace-threat-identification-program-aatip-cancellation-2012) and related questions about Elizondo's alleged programme management role and the [OUSD(I) Security Officer Review of Elizondo 2017](/events/ousdi-security-officer-review-of-elizondo-2017).
