According to the source record, Surviving Death is structured as a detailed examination of selected cases rather than a broad survey. [Leslie Kean](/people/leslie-kean) describes choosing fewer cases so that their evidence could be considered at greater depth.<sup>1</sup> A principal section concerns past-life memories in young children, a field in which the source record reports more than 2,500 documented cases.<sup>2</sup> The James Leininger case is presented as especially important because records of his statements were made before the alleged previous personality, James M. Huston Jr., was identified.<sup>3</sup> The account also discusses Ryan Hammons, whose reported memories of Marty Martyn included more than fifty details subsequently judged accurate by Jim Tucker.<sup>4</sup> These cases are used to examine the evidential importance of early statements, emotional responses, recognitions, behaviours and records created before verification.

The source then broadens its scope to the near-death experience (NDE), [out-of-body experience (OBE)](/topics/out-of-body-experience-obe), after-death communication (ADC) and physical mediumship. It discusses the AWARE study (AWAreness during REsuscitation), described as a multi-hospital investigation of 2,060 cardiac-arrest survivors at fifteen hospitals in the UK, US and Austria.<sup>5</sup> Of the 101 participants completing its interview stages, nine reported NDEs and two described detailed memories of their physical surroundings.<sup>6</sup> The book also reviews Pim van Lommel's prospective Dutch study of 344 cardiac-arrest survivors.<sup>7</sup> Historical mediumship material includes Emil Jensen, an alleged drop-in communicator whose identity was investigated long after his communications through Indridi Indridason.<sup>8</sup> Its central interpretive issue is the contrast between the survival hypothesis and the Living-Agent Psi (LAP) hypothesis, which attributes apparent communications to psychic functioning among living people.<sup>9</sup> Kean concludes that the distinction cannot ultimately be resolved by scientific means alone.<sup>10</sup> The source therefore matters as a synthesis of case reports, prospective research, personal testimony and competing explanations, rather than as a single experimental demonstration.
