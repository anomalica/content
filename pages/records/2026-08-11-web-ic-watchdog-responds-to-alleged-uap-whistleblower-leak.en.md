The [Liberation Times](/organisations/liberation-times) report presents allegations concerning the disclosure of protected testimony attributed to [Dylan Borland]. [Jeremy Corbell](/people/jeremy-corbell) alleged that an [Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI)](/organisations/office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence-odni) source disclosed details of testimony by Borland, whom he described as a UAP whistleblower who had made protected disclosures to the [Office of the Intelligence Community Inspector General (ICIG)](/organisations/office-of-the-intelligence-community-inspector-general-icig) and the [United States Congress](/organisations/united-states-congress).<sup>1</sup> Corbell also said that the disclosed information included claims about an alleged UAP programme codenamed Rubik's Cube.<sup>2</sup> Borland corroborated to Liberation Times the allegation that classified whistleblower testimony had intentionally been disclosed to Corbell.<sup>3</sup> According to the source record, Borland is a former [United States Air Force](/organisations/united-states-air-force-usaf) geospatial intelligence specialist,<sup>4</sup> and later worked as a senior analyst for BAE Systems<sup>5</sup> and Intrepid Solutions, examining video, radar and advanced electro-optical imagery.<sup>6</sup> His reported testimony concerned alleged craft, technologies and legacy UAP programmes operating without congressional oversight, and information he said he had provided to the [All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO)](/organisations/all-domain-anomaly-resolution-office-aaro).

The institutional response is presented as limited and non-confirmatory. According to the report, an ICIG spokesperson declined to confirm whether Borland had provided testimony or whether an investigation was under way.<sup>7</sup> The spokesperson stated that unauthorised disclosure of protected information could result in removal from federal service and referral to the [Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI)](/organisations/federal-bureau-of-investigation-fbi).<sup>8</sup> The report also describes a wider controversy involving the Director's Initiatives Group (DIG), including its inquiry into UAP-related allegations and a private meeting with Matthew Brown at the USA, Virginia, Liberty Crossing Intelligence Campus.<sup>9</sup> Brown said the DIG was more interested in which classified compartments and sub-compartments he had accessed than in the substance of his account.<sup>10</sup> Separately, James E. Erdman III alleged that the [Central Intelligence Agency (CIA)](/organisations/central-intelligence-agency-cia) obstructed the DIG's work and monitored its communications and contacts with whistleblowers.<sup>11</sup> The report records that the ICIG could neither confirm nor deny any pending or ongoing investigation,<sup>12</sup> and that Liberation Times had not independently substantiated allegations concerning Aaron Lukas.<sup>13</sup> The source therefore matters as a record of competing allegations, witness accounts and official refusals to confirm the underlying claims.
