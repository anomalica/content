Peter Khoury said his brother independently retained an account of seeing him levitated and taken by three creatures during the same encounter.<sup>12</sup> He attributed a change in his demeanour during regression to observations by [John Mack](/people/john-mack).<sup>13</sup> The interview also discusses a shin injury that Peter Khoury said had three prongs arranged in a triangle,<sup>14</sup> a pattern the source record associates with the scoop mark phenomenon, and which he said almost completely healed within six hours while leaving a deep scar.<sup>15</sup> Peter Khoury said he founded the UFO Experience Support Association on 1993-04-13.<sup>16</sup> [Chris Ramsay](/people/chris-ramsay) characterised the case, as documented in Hair Of The Alien, as evidence pointing towards [human-alien hybridization](/topics/human-alien-hybridization) and non-human intelligence.<sup>17</sup>
