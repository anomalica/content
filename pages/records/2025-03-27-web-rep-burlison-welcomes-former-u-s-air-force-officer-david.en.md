On 2025-03-27, a press release issued in [D.C. Washington](/places/washington-dc-usa) announced that [David Grusch](/people/david-grusch) had been appointed Special Advisor to [Eric Burlison](/people/eric-burlison), a Republican member of the [United States Congress](/organisations/united-states-congress) representing Missouri's 7th district.<sup>1</sup><sup>2</sup> The release describes Grusch as a decorated former [United States Air Force](/organisations/united-states-air-force) officer and senior civilian intelligence official.<sup>3</sup> In his new advisory role, Grusch is stated to contribute his expertise to Burlison's efforts to promote transparency regarding [Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP)](/topics/unidentified-anomalous-phenomena-uap) and to support Burlison's work on the [Task Force on the Declassification of Federal Secrets](/organisations/task-force-on-the-declassification-of-federal-secrets).<sup>4</sup>

The release provides a summary of Grusch's military and intelligence career. He served 14 years in the Air Force, attaining the rank of Major,<sup>6</sup> and completed a combat tour of duty in [Afghanistan](/places/afghanistan) supporting counter-illicit finance, drug, and arms-trafficking operations alongside the [Joint Special Operations Command (JSOC)](/organisations/joint-special-operations-command-jsoc) and the [International Security Assistance Force (ISAF)](/organisations/international-security-assistance-force-isaf).<sup>7</sup> He subsequently held senior intelligence officer positions at the [National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency (NGA)](/organisations/national-geospatial-intelligence-agency-nga) and the [National Reconnaissance Office (NRO)](/organisations/national-reconnaissance-office-nro),<sup>8</sup> and from 2019 to 2021 officially represented the NRO on the [Unidentified Aerial Phenomena Task Force (UAPTF)](/projects/unidentified-aerial-phenomena-task-force-uaptf).<sup>9</sup>

In 2022, while employed at the NGA, Grusch filed a federal whistleblower complaint — the [David Grusch 2022 whistleblower complaint to the Intelligence Community Inspector General](/documents/david-grusch-2022-whistleblower-complaint-to-the-intelligence-community-inspector-general) — with the [Intelligence Community Inspector General (ICIG)](/organisations/intelligence-community-inspector-general-icig).<sup>10</sup> That complaint was subsequently found to be credible and urgent, qualifying it for reporting to the Congressional intelligence committees.<sup>11</sup> The following year, Grusch gave sworn testimony recorded in the [David Grusch 2023 Congressional testimony on UAP](/documents/david-grusch-2023-congressional-testimony-on-uap) before the [House Oversight & Accountability Committee](/organisations/house-oversight-accountability-committee), in which he alleged that elements of the US government had thwarted Congressional oversight and withheld information from proper Executive Branch officials.<sup>12</sup> The release also notes that in this testimony Grusch alleged the illegal concealment of programmes tied to the recovery and [reverse engineering of craft of non-human origin](/topics/reverse-engineering-of-craft-of-non-human-origin).<sup>13</sup> Grusch had previously provided sworn testimony before the United States Congress on the federal government's UAP activities.<sup>5</sup>

The press release includes a statement attributed to Grusch expressing his intention to help [United States Congress](/organisations/united-states-congress) restore full oversight and to enable the Legislative Branch to investigate and hold accountable those alleged to have broken the law and lied to Congress, the [Executive Office of the President](/organisations/executive-office-of-the-president), the [Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI)](/organisations/office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence-odni), and the [Department of Justice](/organisations/department-of-justice).<sup>14</sup>
