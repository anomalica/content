"Intelligence Officials Say U.S. Has Retrieved Craft of Non-Human Origin" is an investigative article published on 2023-06-05 by [Leslie Kean](/people/leslie-kean), [Ralph Blumenthal](/people/ralph-blumenthal), and [Tim McMillan](/people/tim-mcmillan), based primarily on disclosures by [David Charles Grusch](/people/david-charles-grusch), a former senior intelligence officer turned whistleblower. The article reports Grusch's testimony that the [United States Intelligence Community](/organisations/united-states-intelligence-community) has for decades operated covert programmes in possession of retrieved craft of [Non-Human Intelligence](/topics/non-human-intelligence) origin, and that information about those programmes has been illegally withheld from [United States Congress](/organisations/united-states-congress).<sup>1</sup><sup>2</sup> Grusch simultaneously filed a complaint alleging that he suffered illegal retaliation for making those disclosures.<sup>3</sup> The article notes that other active and retired intelligence officials independently provided corroborating information both on and off the record.<sup>4</sup>

Grusch's career provided the professional basis for his disclosures. A veteran of the [National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency (NGA)](/organisations/national-geospatial-intelligence-agency-nga) and the [National Reconnaissance Office (NRO)](/organisations/national-reconnaissance-office-nro),<sup>5</sup> he served as the NRO's representative to the [Unidentified Aerial Phenomena Task Force](/projects/unidentified-aerial-phenomena-task-force) from 2019 to 2021<sup>6</sup> and subsequently as the NGA's co-lead for [Unidentified Aerial Phenomena (UAP)](/topics/unidentified-aerial-phenomena-uap) analysis and its representative to the same task force from late 2021 to July 2022.<sup>7</sup> His investigation centred on extensive interviews with high-level intelligence officials, some directly involved with the alleged programmes. The article states that his on-the-record statements were cleared for open publication by the [Defense Office of Prepublication and Security Review](/organisations/defense-office-of-prepublication-and-security-review) at the [United States Department of Defense (DoD)](/organisations/united-states-department-of-defense-dod) on 2023-04-04.<sup>10</sup>

The article details Grusch's specific claims about the nature and scale of the alleged recovery operations. He reported that recoveries of partial fragments through to intact vehicles of non-human origin have been conducted for decades by the US government, its allies, and defence contractors,<sup>8</sup> and that analysis of retrieved objects determined they are of exotic origin based on vehicle morphologies, material science testing, and unique atomic arrangements and radiological signatures.<sup>9</sup> Grusch further testified that [reverse engineering of recovered craft of non-human origin](/topics/reverse-engineering-of-recovered-craft-of-non-human-origin) formed part of what he described as a decades-long covert competition with near-peer adversaries to retrieve UAP crash and landing material for asymmetric national advantage.<sup>14</sup> He also alleged that UFO legacy programmes had long been concealed within multiple agencies without appropriate reporting to oversight authorities.<sup>13</sup> Beginning in 2022, Grusch provided Congress with hours of recorded classified information transcribed into hundreds of pages about the materials recovery programme,<sup>15</sup> though the article notes that Congress had not been provided with any physical materials related to wreckage or other non-human objects.<sup>16</sup>

The whistleblower complaint was filed in May 2022 by Grusch's legal representative, [Charles III McCullough](/people/charles-iii-mccullough), senior partner of [Compass Rose Legal Group](/organisations/compass-rose-legal-group) in [Washington D.C.](/places/washington-dc-usa) and the original Inspector General of the Intelligence Community, with the [Office of the Intelligence Community Inspector General (ICIG)](/organisations/office-of-the-intelligence-community-inspector-general-icig).<sup>17</sup> The ICIG found the complaint credible and urgent in July 2022,<sup>18</sup> and a summary was submitted to Director of National Intelligence [Avril Haines](/people/avril-haines), the [Senate Select Committee on Intelligence](/organisations/senate-select-committee-on-intelligence), and the [House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence](/organisations/house-permanent-select-committee-on-intelligence).<sup>19</sup> Grusch also assisted in drafting the UAP language for the [National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2023](/documents/national-defense-authorization-act-for-fiscal-year-2023), spearheaded by Senators [Kirsten Gillibrand](/people/kirsten-gillibrand) and [Marco Rubio](/people/marco-rubio) and signed into law by President [Joe Biden](/people/joe-biden) in December 2022,<sup>12</sup> which tasked the Secretary of Defense with establishing a secure mechanism for reporting sensitive UAP information to defence channels.

The article features corroborating statements from several named sources. [Karl E. Nell](/people/karl-e-nell), a recently retired [United States Army](/organisations/united-states-army) Colonel and former Army liaison to the UAP Task Force who worked alongside Grusch,<sup>20</sup> characterised Grusch as beyond reproach<sup>21</sup> and stated that the assertion concerning a covert terrestrial arms race over eighty years focused on reverse engineering technologies of unknown origin is fundamentally correct.<sup>22</sup> [Jonathan Grey](/people/jonathan-grey), an intelligence officer with a Top-Secret Clearance at the [National Air and Space Intelligence Center (NASIC)](/organisations/national-air-and-space-intelligence-center-nasic), whose focus is UAP analysis,<sup>23</sup> stated on record that the [Non-Human Intelligence](/topics/non-human-intelligence) phenomenon is real<sup>24</sup> and that retrievals of craft of non-human origin are a global rather than solely American phenomenon.<sup>25</sup> [Christopher Mellon](/people/christopher-mellon), who spent nearly twenty years in the [United States Intelligence Community](/organisations/united-states-intelligence-community) and served as Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense for Intelligence,<sup>26</sup> told the article's authors that well-placed current and former officials had shared detailed information with him about the alleged craft-retrieval programme, including its history, governing documents, and a location where a craft was allegedly recovered.<sup>27</sup> Mellon had previously facilitated a briefing on retrievals of unexplained objects for staff of the [Senate Armed Services Committee](/organisations/senate-armed-services-committee) on 2019-10-21.<sup>28</sup> [Garry Nolan](/people/garry-nolan), a professor at [Stanford University](/organisations/stanford-university) who has applied laboratory technologies to the analysis of exotic materials,<sup>29</sup> and Representative [Andre Carson](/people/andre-carson), who presided over the first open Congressional hearing on UAP since 1968 in May 2022,<sup>30</sup> also contributed statements to the article. The [Grusch ICIG whistleblower complaint (2022)](/documents/grusch-icig-whistleblower-complaint-2022) and the associated [Grusch ICIG complaint disclosure event (2022)](/events/grusch-icig-complaint-disclosure-event-2022) are the primary underlying documents the article draws upon.
