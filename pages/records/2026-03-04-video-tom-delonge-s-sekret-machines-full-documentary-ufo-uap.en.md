This source presents [Tom DeLonge](/people/tom-delonge)'s account of establishing [To The Stars Academy of Arts and Science](/organisations/to-the-stars-academy-of-arts-and-science-ttsa) after two years of work, with an intended combination of aerospace, science and entertainment.<sup>1</sup> He described the academy as using films to communicate historic events and forward-thinking ideas, involving the public in scientific discovery, and pursuing an exotic craft with a revolutionary energy source.<sup>2</sup> [Luis Elizondo](/people/luis-elizondo) said that he ran the [Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program (AATIP)](/projects/advanced-aerospace-threat-identification-program-aatip) for nearly the last decade, focusing on unidentified aerial technologies,<sup>3</sup> and that this work led him to conclude that Unidentified Aerial Phenomena (UAP) are real.<sup>4</sup> According to the source record, the academy also intended to explore UAP, [extrasensory perception (ESP)](/topics/extrasensory-perception-esp) and [telepathy](/topics/telepathy).<sup>5</sup>

A substantial section discusses the [2004 USS Nimitz UAP encounter](/events/2004-uss-nimitz-uap-encounter). Chris Mellon said that on 2004-11-14 the [USS Nimitz](/objects/uss-nimitz-cvn-68) was operating off [USA, California, San Diego](/places/california-san-diego-usa), escorted by the [USS Princeton](/objects/uss-princeton-cg-59).<sup>6</sup> He described a small-radar-cross-section unidentified aircraft appearing on the Princeton's radar and heading towards the battle group,<sup>7</sup> while the aviators reportedly observed an object with no wings or exhaust.<sup>8</sup> Mellon described it as white, oblong and approximately 12 metres long,<sup>9</sup> and said it reoriented towards an approaching [F/A-18](/objects/fa-18), performed discrete tumbling manoeuvres, and moved behind the aircraft.<sup>10</sup> The pilots reportedly captured gun-camera footage and infrared imagery,<sup>11</sup> and the object remained near the Nimitz for hours, monitored by the Princeton and an E-2 Hawkeye.<sup>12</sup> Mellon further described an ascent to approximately 24,000 metres followed by a descent to approximately 15 metres above the ocean.<sup>13</sup> He said that he later met one of the pilots and confirmed the account.<sup>14</sup>

The source matters because it connects this case narrative with proposed methods for public participation. Elizondo described planned tools for detecting, analysing, categorising and engineering elusive technologies,<sup>15</sup> a virtual community for real-time detection and triangulation,<sup>16</sup> the release of footage from US government systems,<sup>17</sup> and an app for crowd-sourced aerial-phenomena data.<sup>18</sup> These proposals are presented alongside the academy's broader aerospace, scientific and entertainment remit rather than as findings independently established by the source.
