The NASA-UAP-D010, Mercury Atlas 9 Audio Excerpt, May 15, 1963 records observations attributed to [Gordon Cooper](/people/gordon-cooper) during the [Mercury-Atlas 9 mission](/events/mercury-atlas-9-mission). At one hour and 21 minutes into the mission, Cooper said that particles resembling the space fireflies phenomenon were drifting away from him.<sup>1</sup> He then said that particles were departing from Faith 7 (Mercury-Atlas 9 spacecraft),<sup>2</sup> and described them as drifting towards the rear of the spacecraft, with some visible at a considerable distance.<sup>3</sup> Cooper described their appearance as white to whitish, with an almost green tint, and compared them with fireflies on Earth.<sup>4</sup>

The excerpt also places the observation within Cooper’s changing view of the Earth and sky. He said that the Sun was coming up behind him and that a glow was beginning to appear on the clouds.<sup>5</sup> Later, he described the clouds below as changing colour and becoming lighter.<sup>6</sup> Cooper reported being on the day side of Earth,<sup>7</sup> while also saying that the Sun was not yet fully up and that stars remained observable.<sup>8</sup> He described the Earth below as appearing light,<sup>9</sup> stated that the Sun was still behind him,<sup>10</sup> and characterised the sky above as dark, with stars visible very distinctly.<sup>11</sup> The source matters for UAP research because it preserves a first-hand astronaut account of luminous particles observed in proximity to a spacecraft, together with the environmental descriptions Cooper gave while reporting what he saw. The excerpt therefore documents both the reported appearance and the viewing conditions in which the observation was made.
