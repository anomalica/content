This source reports Pentagon statements concerning [Luis Elizondo](/people/luis-elizondo), the [Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program (AATIP)](/projects/advanced-aerospace-threat-identification-program-aatip) and the 2009 Harry Reid Memo to William Lynn III Regarding AATIP Special Access Program Status. Its central subject is whether the memo changed the Pentagon's position on Elizondo's claimed role in AATIP.<sup>1</sup><sup>2</sup>

According to Pentagon Reinforces Mr. Luis Elizondo Had "No Responsibilities" on AATIP; Senator Harry Reid's 2009 Memo Changes Nothing, the Pentagon maintained that Elizondo had no assigned responsibilities for AATIP while working in the [Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence (OUSD(I))](/organisations/office-of-the-under-secretary-of-defense-for-intelligence-ousdi).<sup>3</sup> The source also reports that the Pentagon said this position applied while he worked in OUSDI up to his resignation, effective 2017-10-04.<sup>4</sup><sup>5</sup>

## The memo and the Pentagon response

[Susan Gough](/people/susan-gough) said that the 2009 memo was authentic.<sup>6</sup> According to the source, the memo is dated 2009-07-24.<sup>7</sup> It outlined [Harry Reid](/people/harry-reid)'s request and reasoning for AATIP to receive [Special Access Program (SAP)](/topics/special-access-program-sap) status, and was written to Deputy Secretary of Defense [William Lynn III](/people/william-lynn-iii).<sup>8</sup>

Susan Gough said that the [United States Department of Defense (DoD)](/organisations/united-states-department-of-defense-dod) received the memo and responded to Reid.<sup>9</sup> She also said that her office could not provide [The Black Vault](/organisations/the-black-vault) with a full copy of the DoD response because its Public Affairs office did not release Congressional correspondence.<sup>10</sup> The source reports that the earlier Pentagon statement, which it says the memo did not change, had been issued to [The Intercept](/organisations/the-intercept) by Pentagon spokesperson [Christopher Sherwood](/people/christopher-sherwood).<sup>11</sup>

The source reports that the memo had previously been released with the names of Elizondo and Hal Puthoff redacted.<sup>12</sup> It further states that one page in the initial release by [KLAS-TV](/organisations/klas-tv) showed those redactions, for reasons described as unknown.<sup>13</sup> [George Knapp](/people/george-knapp) is identified in the source as a journalist from KLAS-TV in [Nevada Las Vegas](/places/nevada-las-vegas-usa).<sup>14</sup> The source refers to the 2018 KLAS-TV Story on Pentagon UFO Study Documents July 25 when discussing the redacted names and says that the reason for the redactions remained unclear.<sup>15</sup>

## Accounts of Elizondo's role

Susan Gough said that the [Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA)](/organisations/defense-intelligence-agency-dia) administered AATIP and that Elizondo was never assigned to the DIA.<sup>16</sup> She also said that he interacted with the DIA office managing AATIP while the programme was ongoing, but did not lead it.<sup>17</sup>

The source reports that Hal Puthoff was established as having worked within AATIP.<sup>18</sup> It identifies him as a co-founder of To The Stars Academy of Arts & Science, where Elizondo was employed.<sup>19</sup> Puthoff offered support in response to the Pentagon's assertions about Elizondo's role.<sup>20</sup>

Puthoff said that, as an [Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program (AAWSAP)](/projects/advanced-aerospace-weapon-system-applications-program-aawsap)/AATIP contractor and senior adviser, he continued to attend meetings, provide briefings, access videos, provide proposed programme plans and meet staff under Elizondo's leadership and responsibility for maintaining continuity until Elizondo resigned.<sup>21</sup> Puthoff also said that the public did not have detailed access to daily activities in Pentagon projects and offices, and that staff outside the relevant work, including Public Affairs personnel, could be unclear about highly classified programmes.<sup>22</sup>

## Chronology and programme administration

According to the source, AAWSAP/AATIP contracted work began in 2008 at the DIA.<sup>23</sup> [LLC Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies](/organisations/llc-bigelow-aerospace-advanced-space-studies) initially won the contract for that work.<sup>24</sup> The source reports that funding for the contracted work was cut entirely in 2012.<sup>25</sup>

The source places Elizondo's alleged start of AATIP leadership in 2010, at OUSDI, when the programme was transferred there.<sup>26</sup> It observes that this account creates a chronology that differs from the 2008 start of contracted work at the DIA, and says it is unclear how Puthoff could have worked under Elizondo's leadership in light of those dates and organisational settings.<sup>27</sup> The source also reports that Puthoff did not return a message concerning that discrepancy.<sup>28</sup>

The source argues that the memo's 2009-07-24 date preceded Elizondo's claimed start of heading AATIP by at least five months, and presents this as not disproving the Pentagon's original statement.<sup>29</sup> Elizondo did not respond to a request for comment from The Black Vault, according to the source.<sup>30</sup>
