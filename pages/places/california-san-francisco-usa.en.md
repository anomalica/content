San Francisco is the city on the northern California coast that recurs, in [Jacques Vallée](/people/jacques-vallee)'s account, as a hub of contactee and New Age activity through the 1970s. Vallée wrote the preface to The Invisible College in the city in March 1975.<sup>1</sup> He attended a Sunday afternoon meeting of about ten members of the Urantia group in San Francisco, led by a woman in her late fifties.<sup>2</sup> Vallée said that a representative of the Esoteric Fraternity told him he did not know Grace Hooper Pettipher of San Francisco and was unsure whether she belonged to the same group.<sup>3</sup> He also noted that at least three separate organisations calling themselves Satanic operated in northern California alone.<sup>4</sup>

Public lectures and press coverage of the same milieu took place in the city as well. Claude Vorilhon gave a public lecture in San Francisco in December 1976 attended by about 150 people who paid $3 each, an event catalogued as the 1976 Vorilhon San Francisco lecture.<sup>5</sup> A reporter named O'Hara, working for the San Francisco Chronicle, went through the city's streets posing a different question to people he met each day.<sup>6</sup> According to Messengers of Deception, a San Francisco Chronicle reporter interviewed people on the streets about contact with UFO beings.<sup>7</sup> On 1976-04-01, Jim Hurtak and [Andrija Puharich](/people/andrija-puharich) appeared together on a talk show broadcast in San Francisco.<sup>8</sup>

The city also has an administrative footnote in Scientology's history: [L. Ron Hubbard](/people/l-ron-hubbard) was officially listed as a patient at Oak Knoll Naval Hospital in San Francisco until December 1945.<sup>9</sup>

San Francisco later featured in commentary on Apollo-era imagery and in Star Wars-themed viral media. [Richard C. Hoagland](/people/richard-c-hoagland) said that the optical phenomenon in AS17-134-20426 (Apollo 17 photograph) resembled a terrestrial sunset over San Francisco, in which a scattered refractive rainbow occurring in the west behind the photographer is reflected back into the camera lens from the east by the earth's atmosphere.<sup>10</sup> [Michael Horn](/people/michael-horn) created the video Death Star over San Francisco, which composites objects from the Star Wars franchise into ordinary San Francisco scenes and has been viewed more than three and a half million times.<sup>11</sup>

[Diana Walsh Pasulka](/people/diana-walsh-pasulka) recorded a research trip through the region: she, Vallée and Robbie Graham drove back from Big Sur to San Francisco and stopped for lunch at a dockside restaurant in Santa Cruz.<sup>12</sup> According to an account given in DEBRIEFED episode 85, featuring Erik Nanstiel, a man named Terry, working in 1969 at a San Francisco venue called The Family Dog at the Great Highway, was told by a stranger that beings existing at a higher vibrational level, with a physical existence but outside the human range of perception, wished to speak with him.<sup>13</sup>

The city also served as a departure point for excursions further afield: about thirty people gathered in USA, California, Mendocino to listen to Timothy Leary tapes, many having driven from San Francisco, USA, California, Berkeley, and USA, California, Stanford University.<sup>14</sup>
