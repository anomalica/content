as a man of compromise who tried to please everyone and avoided confrontation, adding that this inability to stand firm was his undoing in his early confrontation with [Adolf Hitler](/people/adolf-hitler) and the Nazis in Rome.<sup>36</sup> It notes that although he is historically credited with helping thousands of Jews escape, he never spoke out openly against Hitler's anti-Semitism, which made him a target of criticism,<sup>37</sup> and draws the same conclusion about his handling of the Fatima consecration request.<sup>38</sup> On 1952-07-07 he issued a pastoral letter to the people of Russia condemning the errors of "godless Communism" and calling for veneration of the Virgin Mary,<sup>39</sup> and on 1953-09-08 he declared 1954 a Marian year without reference to Fatima.<sup>40</sup> Hesemann judges that Pius XII did his best to fulfil the request to consecrate Russia to the Immaculate Heart of Mary but did not achieve the desired effect.<sup>41</sup>

Pius XII died on 1958-10-09, a year after the Death of Bishop da Silva (1957-10) of Leiria, in what came to be recorded as the Death of Pius XII (1958-10-09).<sup>42</sup> His successor [John XXIII](/people/john-xxiii) went to Castel Gandolfo in August 1959 before deciding to open the Third Secret envelope, unlike Pius XII, who had not done so out of respect for what proved a mistaken reading of a 1960 date.<sup>43</sup> In total, five popes - Pius XII, John XXIII, Paul VI, John Paul I and [John Paul II](/people/john-paul-ii) - kept the Third Secret under lock and key.<sup>44</sup> Sister Lúcia later said that the seer children in the 1917 vision had not known which pope they were shown suffering, since the Virgin Mary had not specified whether it was Benedict XV, Pius XII, Paul VI, or John Paul II.<sup>45</sup> John Paul II regarded himself as the "Fatima pope", a title he considered had originally belonged to Pius XII.<sup>46</sup> Hesemann considers the Fatima message to have influenced Vatican secret policy from Pius XII through to John Paul II,<sup>47</sup> and argues that every twentieth-century pope from Pius XII onward was aware of the Fatima messages.<sup>48</sup> Hesemann has separately suggested that Pius XII's importance may have lain less in spiritual power than in being the one figure able to command the attention of Catholics worldwide, speculating that this amounted to an early experiment in "channelling mental energy".<sup>49</sup> Shortly before her death, Godinho (Mother Superior) wrote to Pius XII on 1954-04-25 asking that her institution be officially designated a convent,<sup>50</sup> and in that letter also related that Jacinta Marto had warned her to prepare for 1972, when sins would cause the pope much pain.<sup>51</sup> Separately, and outside the Fatima material, Jesse Mitchells has said that information about the Magenta UFO crash was back-channelled through Pius XII to [Franklin D. Roosevelt](/people/franklin-d-roosevelt).<sup>52</sup>
