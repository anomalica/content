John Greenewald is the founder of The Black Vault, an archive built from documents obtained through the [Freedom of Information Act (FOIA)](/topics/freedom-of-information-act-foia).<sup>1</sup>

Greenewald has scrutinised claims made about [Luis Elizondo](/people/luis-elizondo)'s role leading the [Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program (AATIP)](/projects/advanced-aerospace-threat-identification-program-aatip). He was initially enthusiastic about [To The Stars Academy of Arts and Science (TTSA)](/organisations/to-the-stars-academy-of-arts-and-science-ttsa) but grew increasingly sceptical when he was unable to verify many of Elizondo's claims about the government's UFO programme through FOIA requests and conversations with Pentagon representatives.<sup>2</sup> Elizondo declined Greenewald's requests for an interview.<sup>3</sup> Greenewald contacted TTSA spokesperson Kari DeLonge, sister of [Tom DeLonge](/people/tom-delonge), seeking further information about Elizondo's involvement in AATIP,<sup>4</sup> asking specifically where within the [United States Department of Defense (DoD)](/organisations/united-states-department-of-defense-dod) Elizondo had worked while running the programme.<sup>5</sup> By the time DeLonge replied, Greenewald had become frustrated at the lack of tangible information about AATIP and Elizondo's role in it.<sup>6</sup> According to the same report, Tim McMillan - who, like Greenewald, has a longtime interest in UFOs - had come to doubt Elizondo's involvement with any government UFO programme.<sup>7</sup>

Greenewald's FOIA requests have also been directed at the department that housed AATIP. The [Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence (OUSDI)](/organisations/office-of-the-under-secretary-of-defense-for-intelligence-ousdi) located eight pages determined responsive to two parts of one such request concerning AATIP and Elizondo.<sup>8</sup> According to Jason Samosa, a separate FOIA request filed by Greenewald returned information indicating either about 3,000 or about 30,000 references in [Barack Obama](/people/barack-obama)'s presidential documents.<sup>9</sup>

Through The Black Vault, Greenewald has reported on further official disclosures. According to the book *In Plain Sight*, Greenewald reported on 2019-09-11 that the [United States Navy (USN)](/organisations/united-states-navy-usn) had released the dates of three officially acknowledged encounters with unidentified phenomena.<sup>10</sup> The same source states that Greenewald reported on 2019-10-18 that the [United States Army](/organisations/united-states-army) had released its Cooperative Research and Development Agreement (CRADA) between To The Stars Academy of Arts & Sciences (TTSA) and US Army.<sup>11</sup>
