Dana White was a Pentagon spokeswoman and Trump administration political appointee who, in comments to [Politico](/organisations/politico) in December 2017, confirmed that the [Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program (AATIP)](/projects/advanced-aerospace-threat-identification-program-aatip) existed and was run by [Luis Elizondo](/people/luis-elizondo).<sup>1</sup>

White made the statement while serving as a Pentagon spokesperson.<sup>1</sup> According to a 2024 account of the Pentagon's UAP-related work, the [Politico](/organisations/politico) article reporting White's confirmation described her as having verified Elizondo's role in the programme.<sup>2</sup>

Her account of the programme was not corroborated by all of her colleagues. [The Pentagon](/organisations/the-pentagon) spokesperson [Christopher Sherwood](/people/christopher-sherwood) said he could not confirm White's statement that AATIP existed and was run by Elizondo.<sup>3</sup>

White left her Pentagon post in 2019. She resigned earlier that year amid an internal probe into charges of misconduct.<sup>4</sup>
