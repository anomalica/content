William Lynn III was a United States Deputy Secretary of Defense at the [United States Department of Defense (DoD)](/organisations/united-states-department-of-defense-dod) in 2009, when he became the recipient of a series of communications from Senator [Harry Reid](/people/harry-reid) concerning the [Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program (AATIP)](/projects/advanced-aerospace-threat-identification-program-aatip).

On 2009-06-24, Reid wrote to Lynn, in correspondence recorded as the Senator Harry Reid Letter to Deputy Secretary of Defense William Lynn III, requesting that the [Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program (AAWSAP)](/projects/advanced-aerospace-weapon-system-applications-program-aawsap) and AATIP be made a DoD [Special Access Program (SAP)](/topics/special-access-program-sap).<sup>1</sup> According to reporting by The Black Vault, a follow-up memo dated 2009-07-24 - recorded as the Harry Reid memo to William Lynn III on Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program (AATIP) Special Access Program status (2009-07-24) - set out Reid's request and his reasoning for it, again addressed to Lynn.<sup>2</sup>

In correspondence recorded separately as the Reid letter to William Lynn III (2009), Reid told Lynn that much progress had been made in identifying several highly sensitive, unconventional aerospace-related findings, and asked that AATIP be designated a "restricted special access program" limited to a few named officials.<sup>3</sup>

Reid's request was denied.<sup>4</sup> According to the Report on the Historical Record of U.S. Government Involvement with Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP), Volume I, Lynn declined to designate AAWSAP/AATIP a DoD Special Access Program, acting on the recommendation of then-Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence [James R. Clapper](/people/james-r-clapper) - set out in the 2009-11-17 Memorandum from James Clapper to the Deputy Secretary of Defense - that such a designation was not justified.<sup>5</sup>
