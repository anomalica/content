Christopher Bledsoe is a Baptist from North Carolina who had worked as a pilot and built a successful construction business before a sighting altered the course of his life.<sup>1</sup> Bledsoe has said that he had a profound [UFO](/topics/unidentified-flying-object-ufo) sighting which he came to interpret as an extension of his own religious tradition.<sup>2</sup>

His congregation did not share this interpretation: church members rejected his account and characterised the experience as demonic.<sup>3</sup> The rejection contributed to several difficult years in which Bledsoe felt alienated from his community, though he later appeared to have reached a settled understanding of what the experience meant to him.<sup>4</sup>

Bledsoe's son, Ryan Bledsoe, hosts a podcast.<sup>5</sup> [Chris Ramsay](/people/chris-ramsay) visited Bledsoe at his home in North Carolina.<sup>6</sup> Ramsay has said that Bledsoe has been in touch with many factions of intelligence agencies in the United States.<sup>7</sup>

Bledsoe is the author of the book Unidentified Flying Object (UFO) of God. According to a 2024 DEBRIEFED interview, the book details an orb dripping an unknown substance onto Bledsoe's driveway.<sup>8</sup> Ramsay has said that this account predates public discussion of angel hair by years.<sup>9</sup>

Ramsay has also recounted that, in the same book, Bledsoe wrote of healing his dog Nellie - who he said was spurting blood across the house - by placing his hands on her, and of healing a Make-A-Wish child with intestinal problems and little time to live in the same way.<sup>10</sup>

Jason Samosa has said that Ryan Bledsoe recounted being told by [Jim Semivan](/people/jim-semivan) that the Bledsoe family are thought to be hybrids, their experiences potentially explained by [human-alien hybridization](/topics/human-alien-hybridization) involving a particular strand of non-human DNA.<sup>11</sup>
