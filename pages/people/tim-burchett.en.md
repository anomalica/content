Tim Burchett is a US representative for Tennessee. Before entering Congress, he was a born-again Christian who owned a trailer repair shop.<sup>1</sup> His congressional district lay near USA, Tennessee, Oak Ridge, home to the nation's uranium stockpile, a former World War II [Manhattan Project](/projects/manhattan-project) "Secret City", and the site of numerous UAP sightings.<sup>2</sup>

Burchett was among the representatives present at the House Hearing on Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (2023-07-26). According to the hearing record, [Anna Paulina Luna](/people/anna-paulina-luna), [Eric Burlison](/people/eric-burlison), Matt Gaetz, James Comer, Andrew Ogles, Nick Langworthy, Jamie Raskin, and Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez were also present alongside him.<sup>3</sup> Glenn Grothman waived Burchett, Luna, Gaetz, Burlison, Ocasio-Cortez, and Ogles onto the subcommittee for the purpose of participating in the hearing,<sup>4</sup> and later thanked Burchett and Luna for bringing the topic of Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP) to the attention of the [United States House Committee on Oversight and Accountability](/organisations/united-states-house-committee-on-oversight-and-accountability).<sup>5</sup> During the proceedings, Burchett entered the Defense Intelligence Reference Document Advanced Space Propulsion Based on Vacuum (Spacetime Metric) Engineering into the hearing record as an unclassified public document.<sup>6</sup> The committee was denied access to a Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility (SCIF) for a confidential discussion during the hearing.<sup>7</sup>

Burchett, Gaetz, Luna, and Burlison visited USA, Florida, Eglin Air Force Base to try to get answers on [Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP)](/topics/unidentified-anomalous-phenomena-uap).<sup>8</sup> The trip followed Gaetz seeking a briefing about the Eglin incident and bringing Burchett and Luna with him.<sup>9</sup> Burchett said that without Gaetz's involvement, he, Luna, and Burlison would still be at Eglin trying to get answers,<sup>10</sup> and said that Gaetz has an excellent legal mind.<sup>11</sup>

Burchett proposed an amendment to the FAA reauthorization that would have required an airline pilot's UAP sighting report made to the FAA to be forwarded to Congress.<sup>12</sup> The amendment was not heard in committee, after he was told the intelligence community did not like it.<sup>13</sup>

Burchett's father served in the [United States Marine Corps (USMC)](/organisations/united-states-marine-corps-usmc) 1st Marine Division,<sup>14</sup> alongside Chesty Puller at the Battle of Peleliu.<sup>15</sup> At the 2023 hearing, Burchett met a person who had travelled from Denmark to attend.<sup>16</sup> He said some members of [United States Congress](/organisations/united-states-congress) had confided in him that they had had [Unidentified Flying Object (UFO)](/topics/unidentified-flying-object-ufo) sightings of their own while wishing to remain anonymous.<sup>17</sup> Luis Elizondo said Burchett was particularly alarmed by the connection between UAP and US nuclear capabilities.<sup>18</sup> [Phil McGraw](/people/phil-mcgraw) has said he discussed the UAP subject with [Donald Trump](/people/donald-trump), Stephen Miller, Burchett, and Luna.<sup>19</sup>
