Ben Rich was an American aerospace engineer who served as president of [Lockheed Martin Skunk Works](/organisations/lockheed-martin-skunk-works), [Lockheed Martin](/organisations/lockheed-martin)'s secretive Advanced Development Projects division, during the 1990s and was responsible for the company's modern stealth aircraft.<sup>1</sup> He died in 1995, having become known for dropping mischievous hints that the United States had achieved a major technological breakthrough that was still being kept under wraps.<sup>2</sup>

Aviation researcher Jim Goodall befriended Rich during his time as the division's former director.<sup>3</sup> Goodall said that Rich's last comment to him before he died was: "Jim, we have things out in the desert that are 50 years beyond what you can comprehend. Not what you think we can make in 50 years but what you can comprehend. And if you've seen movies like Star Trek and Star Wars, we've been there, done that or decided it wasn't worth the effort."<sup>4</sup>

According to In Plain Sight, Rich gave an address in March 1993 to a small group of alumni at the University of California Los Angeles engineering school, an occasion now recorded as the 1993-03 Ben Rich UCLA address.<sup>5</sup> According to the same source, Jan Harzan and aerospace engineer T.L. Keller, who were among the group present, attested that Rich admitted the United States had figured out how to achieve interstellar travel and, without explanation, said there was an error in the equations.<sup>6</sup> Harzan recalled that when Rich ended the talk, he showed a slide portraying a black disc flying off into space.<sup>7</sup> Harzan said Rich's final words to the packed auditorium were: "We've discovered what it is, and we now have the technology to travel to the stars" - which Harzan understood to mean taking extra-terrestrials home.<sup>8</sup> Harzan asked Rich once or twice how the technology worked, and Rich did not really want to go there.<sup>9</sup> Afterwards, Harzan introduced himself and asked again; Rich replied "How does ESP work?", and when Harzan answered that all points in space and time are connected, said "That's how it works."<sup>10</sup> [Ross Coulthart](/people/ross-coulthart) has suggested that believers in the extra-terrestrial explanation for Rich's remarks may have been too credulous in taking him literally, proposing that the United States might have wanted strategic rivals Russia and China to believe it held such an advanced edge, and that Rich may have been spreading disinformation through a prominent aerospace journalist.<sup>11</sup>

Rich made a similar mischievous remark to John Andrews, a stealth aircraft model-kit designer for the Testor Corporation.<sup>12</sup> According to In Plain Sight, in a July 1986 letter Andrews asked Rich to clarify whether he believed in both man-made [UFOs](/topics/unidentified-flying-object-ufo) and extra-terrestrial UFOs.<sup>13</sup> In his reply, preserved as the 1986-07 Ben Rich letter to John Andrews, Rich wrote: "Dear John, Yes, I'm a believer in both categories. I feel everything is possible. Many of our man-made UFOs are Un Funded Opportunities."<sup>14</sup>

According to Jesse Mitchells, Rich wrote about an international corporate board of directors that deals with the UFO issue.<sup>15</sup> Mitchells described this board as very similar both to the Maynard Consortium, described in the document Secret Machines, and to the Caroline Group encountered by [Thomas Townsend Brown](/people/thomas-townsend-brown).<sup>16</sup> Mitchells said that late in his life, Rich spoke about UFOs and lamented that the issue was governed by an international corporate board, hoping instead that it would be brought under the umbrella of the American intelligence and aerospace world.<sup>17</sup> In Mitchells's characterisation, Rich was at the tip of the spear in building reconnaissance aircraft for the [Central Intelligence Agency (CIA)](/organisations/central-intelligence-agency-cia) and advanced American defence technology.<sup>18</sup> Mitchells said Rich stated there was something international above him that governed the UFO issue.<sup>19</sup>
