Elon Musk is the founder of SpaceX, the aerospace manufacturer and launch provider. He does not otherwise appear as a direct subject in Anomalica's UAP-related source material; the available claims about him are second-hand accounts and opinions offered by others, chiefly the retired US Navy pilot [David Fravor](/people/david-fravor).

Fravor said that Musk's SpaceX delivered a project that had originally been contracted to [Boeing](/organisations/boeing), completing it in less time and for half the cost.<sup>1</sup> Fravor also related an account of a SpaceX rocket test launched from Kwajalein Atoll in the Marshall Islands, in which a fuel line rupture caused the rocket to run out of fuel before reaching full altitude and fall back down near the atoll.<sup>2</sup> Fravor said that Musk was unhappy about the failed test and blamed the engineer responsible.<sup>3</sup>

Fravor also passed on hearsay from a friend who had done government contract work and worked with SpaceX. According to Fravor, this friend said Musk owns a property of approximately 160 hectares near Texas Waco.<sup>4</sup> The same friend reportedly told Fravor that SpaceX bought out a tool-and-die machine shop supplying high-precision rocket parts after its owner was close to going out of business, with the deal completed within an hour or two of meeting Musk.<sup>5</sup>

In a separate context, Erik Nanstiel said that mapping a set of caverns in Illinois would require funding from someone like Musk, who could write a cheque for the work without missing the money.<sup>6</sup> This remark was offered as an illustration of the scale of funding required rather than a claim about any involvement by Musk, and no further connection between him and the topic of deep underground military bases is recorded in the available material.
