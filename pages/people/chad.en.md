Chad is a cousin of [David Fravor](/people/david-fravor), the former US Navy pilot associated with the 2004 USS Nimitz encounter. Chad and another cousin, [Ray](/people/ray), lived near Fravor in the same neighbourhood, growing up together as part of an Italian family.<sup>1</sup> Fravor has said that he, Chad, Ray, and two further family members grew up alongside one another and that he considers them like brothers.<sup>2</sup>

Fravor said that he would drop everything and travel immediately if either Ray or Chad ever needed him, describing the closeness of the relationship.<sup>3</sup>

According to Fravor, Chad recorded the video footage of the object encountered during the incident. Fravor said that Chad subsequently reviewed the footage's electro-optical and infrared imaging modes and found no visual signs of propulsion.<sup>4</sup>
