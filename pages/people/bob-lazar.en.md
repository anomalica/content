Bob Lazar is an American physicist who came to public attention in 1989 when he gave an interview to [George Knapp](/people/george-knapp) on Las Vegas television station [KLAS-TV](/organisations/klas-tv), asserting that he had been employed to work on a recovered extraterrestrial craft at a classified facility known as the [S-4 Facility](/places/s-4-facility), situated adjacent to [Papoose Lake](/places/papoose-lake) south of [Area 51](/places/area-51) in Nevada.<sup>1</sup> Prior to coming forward, Lazar had been employed at [Los Alamos National Laboratory](/organisations/los-alamos-national-laboratory), a connection that was subsequently substantiated when Knapp rebutted official denials of Lazar's time there, including locating an old news article placing him at the facility.<sup>2</sup> Independent accounts have corroborated his employment and his involvement in propulsion-related work at Los Alamos.<sup>3</sup> By his own account, before any of his disclosures, Lazar had been a UFO sceptic who declared he would not admit such craft were real under any circumstances.<sup>4</sup>

Lazar states he was recruited to the [USA, Nevada, S4](/places/nevada-s4-usa) programme through the influence of physicist [Edward Teller](/people/edward-teller), whom he credits with recommending him by describing a promising young man at Los Alamos who deserved an opportunity.<sup>5</sup> He has also speculated that the programme may have sought someone unconventional who would not be constrained by established protocols, given a perceived lack of progress.<sup>6</sup> At [S-4](/places/s-4-facility), Lazar was paired with a trainer known only as [Barry](/people/barry), and the two were assigned to work on the craft's power system and [Anti-Gravity Propulsion](/concepts/anti-gravity-propulsion) system.<sup>7</sup> Their stated directives were twofold: first, to replicate the craft's propulsion system using available materials,<sup>8</sup> and second, to develop a means of disabling the system remotely.<sup>9</sup> Neither task carried a budget limit. During the assignment, Lazar was permitted to read three classified briefing documents designated Galileo, Sidekick, and Looking Glass.<sup>10</sup> He has stated his view that gravity propulsion was the most strategically significant avenue of research, reasoning that mastery of gravity would yield control over [time dilation](/topics/time-dilation) as a consequence.<sup>11</sup>

Among the material Lazar encountered in briefing papers at S4 was content describing how [Greys (extraterrestrial beings)](/topics/greys-extraterrestrial-beings) reportedly regarded humans as containers, and that religion had been introduced to human populations to prevent damage to those containers.<sup>12</sup> He has also described being given an opaque yellow fluid to drink at S4, explained to him as an immune-system booster necessitated by the presence of materials of unknown composition and unknown biological interaction.<sup>13</sup> Lazar maintains he was fully clear-headed during all his time at the facility. He states the programme's primary method of controlling personnel was not coercion or physical silencing but the deliberate insertion of false and individualised information into briefing documents, allowing administrators to identify the source of any leak.<sup>14</sup> His trainer [Barry](/people/barry) separately corroborated the existence of this practice.

Following his disclosure through [George Knapp](/people/george-knapp), Lazar was contacted by [Dennis Mariani](/people/dennis-mariani), a security figure from S4 whom Lazar describes as operating on a different level from ordinary guards and as a figure many researchers subsequently sought out.<sup>15</sup> Mariani arranged a meeting at the [USA, Nevada, Las Vegas, Union Plaza Hotel](/places/nevada-las-vegas-union-plaza-hotel-usa), and Lazar brought witnesses including [Gene Huff](/people/gene-huff) to observe.<sup>16</sup> At the meeting, however, Mariani refused to look at or acknowledge Lazar in any way, ignoring him entirely even when directly addressed.<sup>17</sup> Lazar also observed familiar faces he recognised as S4 security personnel in the vicinity. A separate security figure, [Chuck Payne](/people/chuck-payne), was described by Lazar as a conspicuously outgoing and extroverted guard at S4, notable among a generally tight-lipped security contingent for greeting personnel by name.<sup>18</sup> Lazar disclosed Payne's name publicly for the first time in a 2026 interview, having withheld it as one of several details he retains to test the authenticity of those who claim inside knowledge of the programme.<sup>19</sup>

Lazar has stated his motivation for speaking out, despite having taken an oath, was a belief that the technology he encountered could benefit all of humanity and ought not to be withheld.<sup>20</sup> He attributes the absence of further whistleblowers primarily to fear rather than to individuals being physically prevented from speaking.<sup>21</sup> Lazar has also examined material from the [Billy Meier Unidentified Flying Object (UFO) contact case](/topics/billy-meier-unidentified-flying-object-ufo-contact-case), and reportedly stated that the craft associated with [Billy Meier](/people/billy-meier), known as the sports model or beam ship, was an identical match to craft he had direct familiarity with, though he and [Chris Ramsay](/people/chris-ramsay) have both expressed scepticism about later [Billy Meier](/people/billy-meier) material.<sup>22</sup> Those who have met Lazar in person, including retired US Navy aviator [David Fravor](/people/david-fravor), have described him as direct, intellectually capable, and as someone for whom public attention on the UFO subject has been a disruption rather than a pursuit.<sup>23</sup> Lazar has also indicated awareness of and respect for the disclosures made by [David Grusch](/people/david-grusch), and has expressed an acquaintance with pilot and UFO researcher [John Lear](/people/john-lear), whose broader claims he has stated he found implausible. He has appeared publicly alongside [Jeremy Corbell](/people/jeremy-corbell) and [David Fravor](/people/david-fravor) at the [McMinnville Unidentified Flying Object (UFO) Festival](/events/mcminnville-unidentified-flying-object-ufo-festival).
