Phil McGraw is an American television personality and psychologist, known for hosting a long-running daytime talk programme; he has hosted the show for 25 years.<sup>1</sup> He has practised as a psychologist for close to 50 years<sup>2</sup> and has held a pilot's licence since he was 16.<sup>3</sup> Earlier in his career he worked extensively in litigation as a trial scientist,<sup>4</sup> during which he observed that litigation clients would sometimes hide a key document in plain sight by burying it among thousands of others so it was lost in the volume.<sup>5</sup> He serves on the Presidential Commission for Religious Liberty,<sup>6</sup> a body that involves the US Department of Justice.<sup>7</sup> In 2026 McGraw produced televised commentary on [Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP)](/topics/ufos-uaps), reviewing declassified government files and interviewing figures including whistleblower [David Charles Grusch](/people/david-charles-grusch).

Credible witnesses, including military pilots, commercial pilots and people with trained instrumentation, made observations of [Unidentified Flying Object (UFO)](/topics/ufos-uaps) sightings that were being suppressed.<sup>8</sup> Such witnesses were sometimes threatened with prison time,<sup>9</sup> substantial fines,<sup>10</sup> or prosecution under the Espionage Act.<sup>11</sup> Documents still due for release contain gun camera footage<sup>12</sup> and radar tracks<sup>13</sup> relevant to these sightings. McGraw has likened this pattern of suppression to a psychological operation of the kind used in past war efforts, in which the media apparatus was said to have been co-opted to fabricate atrocities and build public support.<sup>14</sup> He considers there to be a fragmentation of UFO-related information within the US government,<sup>15</sup> and has said that some of those controlling it treat the President of the United States as though he were a temporary employee not given full candour.<sup>16</sup> McGraw recounted that in 1947, Lieutenant General Nathan Twining told the US Army Air Forces that the phenomenon under investigation was "something real and not visionary or fictitious".<sup>17</sup> The US Air Force denied for decades that this assessment even existed.<sup>18</sup> In 1949 a classified conference was convened at Los Alamos, New Mexico, to address a wave of unexplained sightings over America's nuclear weapon sites.<sup>19</sup> More recently, there have been sightings of anomalous phenomena over the Pantex Plant nuclear site in Amarillo, Texas,<sup>20</sup> and five cities with nuclear concentrations have had a high incidence of verified UFO sightings over nuclear installations.<sup>21</sup> In 1953, the CIA's [Robertson Panel](/projects/robertson-panel) recommended a public education campaign built on publicising already-debunked cases while saying nothing about unexplained ones.<sup>22</sup> A later directive, JNAP 146E, extended flight restrictions to retired pilots after they were found to fall outside the jurisdiction of an earlier directive, JNAP 146,<sup>23</sup> and imposed the Espionage Act on the pilots it covered.<sup>24</sup>

[Donald Trump](/people/donald-trump) signed an executive order to declassify and disclose UFO-related information to the public,<sup>25</sup> which McGraw said followed Trump's decision that it was time for transparency on disclosure.<sup>26</sup> A fourth tranche of UFO files was subsequently released,<sup>27</sup> which McGraw reviewed before it became public.<sup>28</sup> He concluded from that review that there has been a massive cover-up,<sup>29</sup> and that there is no question unexplained phenomena are occurring in Earth's atmosphere.<sup>30</sup> By the time a fifth tranche had been released, five tranches of UAP material were in the public domain in total.<sup>31</sup> McGraw considers the released videos, radar tracks and infrared material to be heavily degraded versions of what exists, noting that gun camera video is normally crystal clear when it is meant to be.<sup>32</sup> He believes Trump intends genuine transparency and that the degraded state of the releases does not originate with him, but that material becomes garbled once it enters the bureaucratic apparatus,<sup>33</sup> and that the redactions and cherry-picking evident in the disclosures so far indicate an ongoing tug-of-war over the full truth of what the government knows.<sup>34</sup>
