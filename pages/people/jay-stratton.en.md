Jay Stratton is a former US government intelligence officer who spent sixteen years investigating [Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena](/concepts/unidentified-anomalous-phenomena), the longest such tenure that [Luis Elizondo](/people/luis-elizondo) was aware of.<sup>20</sup> He is credited with helping establish two successive government UAP programmes: the [Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program](/matters/advanced-aerospace-weapon-system-applications-program), which he stated he helped create and which later evolved into the [Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program](/organisations/advanced-aerospace-threat-identification-program) (AATIP),<sup>1</sup> and ultimately the [UAP Task Force](/organisations/uap-task-force), of which he became the first director.<sup>18</sup> Elizondo, writing in his 2024 memoir, credits Stratton's work as essential to enabling UAP disclosure.<sup>24</sup>

Stratton's involvement with government UAP investigation began in the context of AATIP, run out of [the Pentagon](/organisations/the-pentagon). In early 2009, he and a colleague, [Rosemary Caine](/people/rosemary-caine), visited Elizondo's office unexpectedly to recruit him as a senior intelligence officer for a programme at the [Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA)](/organisations/defense-intelligence-agency-dia) requiring counterintelligence and security expertise.<sup>2</sup> Stratton had already been active on the UAP file before Elizondo's involvement, having conducted an early investigation into the [2004 USS Nimitz UAP encounter](/events/2004-uss-nimitz-uap-encounter) prior to Elizondo joining AATIP.<sup>3</sup> He subsequently contacted [David Fravor](/people/david-fravor) in 2009 to investigate the Tic Tac event as part of the AATIP work at the Pentagon.<sup>4</sup> He also wrote a detailed AATIP report on the Tic Tac incident.<sup>5</sup> Alongside Elizondo and [John Robert](/people/john-robert), Stratton later moved the remnants of AATIP away from the DIA and into Elizondo's portfolio within the [Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence (OUSDI)](/organisations/office-of-the-under-secretary-of-defense-for-intelligence-ousdi).<sup>6</sup> He drafted the programme's appropriations language framed under intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance (ISR) to conceal its UAP focus from those not privy to the investigation,<sup>7</sup> and secured USD 10 million in new AATIP funding from Senator Harry Reid.<sup>8</sup>

Stratton is attributed with coining the term "UAP" as a replacement for "UFO", on the basis that the new terminology made institutional conversations about the phenomenon easier.<sup>9</sup> In 2015, he received a series of emails from senior leaders at the Navy's [Fleet Forces Command](/organisations/fleet-forces-command) in Norfolk, Virginia, describing UAP incursions involving the aircraft carrier [USS Theodore Roosevelt](/objects/uss-theodore-roosevelt).<sup>10</sup> He forwarded those emails to Elizondo and spoke with him about the events,<sup>11</sup> and subsequently spent weeks drafting an operation plan code-named [OPLAN Interloper](/matters/oplan-interloper) in response to twenty-two UAP incidents involving the Theodore Roosevelt Carrier Strike Group.<sup>12</sup> Elizondo and Stratton submitted OPLAN Interloper to the [Joint Chiefs of Staff](/organisations/joint-chiefs-of-staff) via an Alternate Compensatory Control Measures process, routing around what they regarded as a compromised [Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence (OUSDI)](/organisations/office-of-the-under-secretary-of-defense-for-intelligence-ousdi).<sup>13</sup> Before the end of 2016, Stratton informed Elizondo that the Joint Chiefs had rejected assigning OPLAN Interloper an Alternative Compensatory Control Measure designation.<sup>14</sup>

Following that setback, Stratton, Elizondo, and [Christopher K. Mellon](/people/christopher-k-mellon) concluded that the only viable path to changing the Pentagon's handling of UAP was to engage Congress, and that reaching Congress required involving the press.<sup>15</sup> The three devised a disclosure plan in which Elizondo would resign from government and go public, while Stratton would remain inside government to exploit the resulting momentum for briefings and to run whatever successor programme to AATIP emerged.<sup>16</sup> After the public disclosure, Stratton was tasked by Navy intelligence leadership to quietly build a whole-of-government interagency UAP task force with greater authorities than AATIP had possessed,<sup>17</sup> drawing representatives from the FBI, National Reconnaissance Office (NRO), NASA, and the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA). The Secretary of Defense subsequently announced the [UAP Task Force](/organisations/uap-task-force) and named Stratton as its first director.<sup>18</sup> Once the task force became official, what Elizondo refers to as the [UAP Legacy Programs](/matters/uap-legacy-programs) began creating bureaucratic obstacles for Stratton and the task force behind the scenes.<sup>19</sup>

Stratton also conducted fieldwork at [Skinwalker Ranch](/places/skinwalker-ranch) in Utah as part of AATIP operations. On his first visit, he reported seeing a hovering delta-shaped UAP directly above him.<sup>21</sup> He coined the term "[Jay Stratton Hitchhiker Effect](/matters/jay-stratton-hitchhiker-effect)" to describe the reported phenomenon of anomalous experiences following witnesses home from the ranch after their visits.<sup>22</sup> His own family reportedly experienced a range of such phenomena following his July 2009 visit, according to accounts documented in the [Skinwalkers at the Pentagon Book](/documents/skinwalkers-at-the-pentagon-book), where Stratton is pseudonymously identified as "Axelrod".<sup>23</sup> He also reported personally observing a translucent entity at the ranch, which he described as reminiscent of the Predator film character, an account he stated was independently corroborated by a colleague. Stratton spoke publicly about his personal paranormal experiences at [Jay Stratton Skinwalker Ranch Sightings](/matters/jay-stratton-skinwalker-ranch-sightings) at a UAP conference in 2023-03. He retired from government service having, by Elizondo's account, spent more time investigating UAP than any other individual Elizondo knew of.<sup>20</sup>
