David Grush is a whistleblower on [Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP)](/topics/unidentified-anomalous-phenomena-uap) who came out publicly in June 2023<sup>1</sup> and testified before the [United States Congress](/organisations/united-states-congress) in July 2023, in what is recorded as David Grush's 2023 congressional testimony on Unidentified Aerial Phenomena (UAP).<sup>2</sup> Grush has never mentioned [Immaculate Constellation](/projects/immaculate-constellation).<sup>3</sup>

On 2024-11-13, Grush appeared again at the 2024 United States congressional hearing on UAPs (13 November). He testified that the US military and intelligence community possess a large amount of visual and other sensor information on UAP, including still photos, video, and other sensor data, which they have held for a long time.<sup>4</sup> Some of this material, he said, is high resolution, unlike previously released blurry footage.<sup>5</sup> Grush said he has been told there are hundreds, possibly thousands, of visuals, graphics, videos and photos of UAP held by the government.<sup>6</sup> At the same hearing, Grush denied being familiar with a rumoured [United States Department of Defense (DoD)](/organisations/united-states-department-of-defense-dod) human-genetic hybridisation programme when asked about it by Lauren Boebert.<sup>7</sup>

Chris Ramsay has said that Grush gave up his pension and put his own credibility on the line by publicly stating that non-human biologics and craft had been recovered and were being studied.<sup>8</sup> [Bob Lazar](/people/bob-lazar) has said he respects Grush for coming forward publicly.<sup>9</sup> No one else from the programme Grush described has come forward publicly.<sup>10</sup>

According to Ross Coulthart, Grush said it was highly significant to him that the 1971 US-Soviet Unidentified Aerial Phenomena (UAP) hotline agreement (Strategic Arms Limitation Talks (SALT) talks) document had been declassified, allowing him to discuss it publicly since it was in the public domain.<sup>11</sup> Coulthart has also said that in Grush's presentation on the steps of Congress, Grush mentioned sentient plasmoids among the life forms he was aware of, when asked by a reporter about life forms.<sup>12</sup> Coulthart has noted that Britain's Condign Report inferred findings about plasma similar to Grush's mention of sentient plasmoids, some 20 to 30 years earlier.<sup>13</sup> Coulthart has further said that Grush talked about agreements he was able to discuss under Defense Office of Prepublication and Security Review (DOPSR) clearance, potentially involving foreign adversary powers or [Non-Human Intelligence](/topics/non-human-intelligence) regarding UAP.<sup>14</sup>

Jesse Mitchells has said that the United States government has been conducting covert reverse-engineering programmes on [Unidentified Flying Object (UFO)](/topics/unidentified-flying-object-ufo)s for approximately 70 to 80 years.<sup>15</sup>
