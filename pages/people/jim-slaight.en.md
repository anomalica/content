Jim Slaight, call sign "Clean", was the Weapon Systems Officer in [David Fravor](/people/david-fravor)'s back seat during the [2004 USS Nimitz UAP encounter](/events/2004-uss-nimitz-uap-encounter).<sup>1</sup> He had a reputation as an experienced and effective Weapon Systems Officer, known as the "warheads on foreheads" guy for the precision with which he dropped his bombs.<sup>2</sup>

During the encounter, Slaight saw the giant Tic Tac craft from above.<sup>3</sup> According to Imminent: Inside the Pentagon's Hunt for UFOs, all four pilots noticed a bizarre object darting back and forth over the whitecaps, about 15 metres above the water.<sup>4</sup> As Fravor and Slaight closed on the object, David Fravor said it disappeared over the horizon in a split second, a type of performance neither pilot had previously encountered.<sup>5</sup>

Slaight described the craft as having defined edges but with a fuzzy-looking border around it,<sup>6</sup> likening the border to heat waves rising off a hot paved road, comparable to the way a carrier deck looks when viewed across it in the Persian Gulf.<sup>7</sup> He watched it complete a tight circle around Fravor's aircraft, then stop, hover for a couple of seconds, and shoot off.<sup>8</sup> Slaight said it rifled out of sight in a split second, as though fired from a rifle, with no gradual acceleration or spooling-up period, and said no human could have withstood that kind of acceleration.<sup>9</sup>

Slaight and his pilot initially suspected the ocean disturbance beneath the object was a submarine taking part in the exercise, a possibility later ruled out by the [USS Nimitz (CVN-68)](/objects/uss-nimitz-cvn-68)'s intelligence officer during a post-flight debriefing.<sup>10</sup> The Tic Tac appeared to know the intended combat air patrol rendezvous point of the two [F/A-18](/objects/fa-18) Hornets, a point sixty miles away, and reached it within seconds of leaving Fravor and Slaight.<sup>11</sup> According to Imminent: Inside the Pentagon's Hunt for UFOs, both [Chad Underwood](/people/chad-underwood) and Fravor and Slaight reported the craft's instant disappearance over the horizon.<sup>12</sup>
