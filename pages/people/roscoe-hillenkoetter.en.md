Roscoe Hillenkoetter was a Rear Admiral in the US Navy who, by May 1947, was settling into his new post as Director of Central Intelligence in [D.C. Washington](/places/district-of-columbia-washington-usa)<sup>1</sup>, a position that became the [Central Intelligence Agency (CIA)](/organisations/central-intelligence-agency-cia) later that year<sup>2</sup>. He had previously commanded the battleship USS Missouri<sup>3</sup> and was wounded aboard a vessel during the attack on Pearl Harbor at Hawaii Pearl Harbor on 1941-12-07<sup>4</sup>. A major priority for him in his new intelligence role was fixing the failures that had allowed the Japanese to attack Pearl Harbor undetected<sup>5</sup>.

Hillenkoetter later joined the Board of Directors of the [National Investigations Committee on Aerial Phenomena (NICAP)](/organisations/national-investigations-committee-on-aerial-phenomena-nicap)<sup>6</sup>, becoming one of three former navy admirals on its board barely a year after leaving the CIA directorship<sup>7</sup>.

Hillenkoetter said it was imperative that we learn what [UFOs](/topics/unidentified-flying-object-ufo) are and where they come from<sup>8</sup>. In 1960, he said it was time for the truth to be brought out in open hearings before [Congress](/organisations/united-states-congress)<sup>9</sup>, and he later told Congress that, behind the scenes, high-ranking air force officers were soberly concerned about UFOs, but that official secrecy and ridicule had led many citizens to believe the unknown flying objects were nonsense<sup>10</sup>. By then a former CIA Director, he pushed for congressional hearings on the UAP issue at a time when the [United States Air Force (USAF)](/organisations/united-states-air-force-usaf) was publicly debunking most UAP sightings while privately treating the phenomenon seriously<sup>11</sup>.

Jacques Vallée has speculated that Hillenkoetter could have been lending credibility to the Martian Conspiracy stratagem by deliberately promoting the extraterrestrial theory of UFOs<sup>12</sup>. [Harry Turner](/people/harry-turner) said that many intelligence officers associated with the UFO problem, including Hillenkoetter, had stated that the US government knew UFOs were extraterrestrial but was withholding this fact from the public<sup>13</sup>.
