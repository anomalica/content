Ralph Blumenthal is a contributor and former staff reporter for [The New York Times](/organisations/the-new-york-times).<sup>1</sup> For several years he was quietly working on a biography of the late [John Mack](/people/john-mack), a psychiatrist at [Harvard University](/organisations/harvard-university) who treated and extensively interviewed people claiming to have been abducted by or to have encountered aliens.<sup>2</sup>

[Luis Elizondo](/people/luis-elizondo), after leaving To The Stars Academy's announcement period, travelled to Philadelphia to meet again with [Leslie Kean](/people/leslie-kean), this time also with her colleague Blumenthal.<sup>3</sup> During that visit, the three noticed two individuals with tight military-style haircuts observing them on the street, whom Elizondo believed were most likely an [Air Force Office of Special Investigations (AFOSI)](/organisations/air-force-office-of-special-investigations-afosi) surveillance team.<sup>4</sup>

In the comments section of the 2020-07-24 New York Times story on alleged US retrieval of alien materials, a reader from San Francisco asked Blumenthal whether the government, or people closely associated with it, had successfully conspired for decades to suppress evidence of alien visitation; Blumenthal responded that such an assumption might not be far off.<sup>5</sup> Blumenthal has since walked back from an earlier claim that materials recovered from [UAP](/topics/unidentified-anomalous-phenomena-uap) were stored in Las Vegas, admitting that no materials had in fact been provided.<sup>6</sup> UFO crash retrievals are a classified subject.<sup>7</sup> According to Ross Coulthart, in an interview on Project Unity, Blumenthal and Kean indicated that they believed there was substance to allegations that the US government had recovered alien spacecraft or fragments of alien spacecraft.<sup>8</sup>

In 2023, [David Charles Grusch](/people/david-charles-grusch) provided the Defense Office of Prepublication and Security Review at the [United States Department of Defense (DoD)](/organisations/united-states-department-of-defense-dod) with the information he intended to disclose to Kean and Blumenthal.<sup>9</sup> Grusch's on-the-record statements were cleared for open publication on 2023-04-04 and 2023-04-06, according to documents provided to Kean and Blumenthal.<sup>10</sup> A representative of the House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence told Kean and Blumenthal in March 2023 that committee members were not able to comment on the content of a complaint or confirm the identity of a complainant.<sup>11</sup>
