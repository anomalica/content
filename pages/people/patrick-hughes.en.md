Patrick Hughes was a Lieutenant General who served as Director of the [Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA)](/organisations/defense-intelligence-agency-dia). During his tenure, [Thomas Wilson](/people/thomas-wilson) served as the agency's deputy director and Assistant Joint Chief of Staff for Intelligence (J-2).<sup>1</sup> Wilson later testified that Hughes was his direct superior.<sup>2</sup>

In April 1997, Hughes was present in a Pentagon conference room when [Steven Greer](/people/steven-greer), [Willard Miller](/people/willard-miller) and [Edgar Mitchell](/people/edgar-mitchell) gave a talk, also attended by Admiral Mike Crawford; the meeting is recorded as the 1997-04 Pentagon briefing by Greer, Miller and Mitchell, held at [the Pentagon](/organisations/the-pentagon).<sup>3</sup>

Hughes invited Greer and Miller back to the DIA in 1998 to brief him further on UFOs.<sup>4</sup> The follow-up invitation came months after the April 1997 briefing.<sup>5</sup>

According to Greer, Hughes responded to the briefing by producing a doll modelled on the extra-terrestrial from the Steven Spielberg film that he kept on his shelf, saying: "I have no doubt that what you are sharing is true, but I have made inquiries through channels in my agency and nobody can tell me anything! In fact, all I have gotten is this!"<sup>6</sup>

Journalist [Ross Coulthart](/people/ross-coulthart) has said that, if Greer's account is accurate, publicly naming both Wilson and Hughes sent a discouraging signal to other officials who might otherwise have considered disclosure, since it suggested Greer could not be trusted to keep confidences.<sup>7</sup> Coulthart has also said he suspects the two DIA officials involved in the 1997 meeting were more interested in UAPs than they publicly acknowledged.<sup>8</sup>
