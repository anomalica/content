Christopher Sherwood is a Pentagon spokesperson who said the Navy [F/A-18](/objects/fa-18) cockpit videos showing unidentified aerial phenomena were released "for research purposes... and not for general public release."<sup>1</sup> He also told reporters that three [Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP)](/topics/unidentified-anomalous-phenomena-uap) videos had been cleared for research purposes only, not for public dissemination.<sup>2</sup>

Sherwood is most prominently associated with Pentagon statements disputing [Luis Elizondo](/people/luis-elizondo)'s role in the [Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program (AATIP)](/projects/advanced-aerospace-threat-identification-program-aatip). He told Keith Kloor unequivocally that Elizondo had no responsibilities regarding AATIP while working in the [Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence (OUSDI)](/organisations/office-of-the-under-secretary-of-defense-for-intelligence-ousdi)<sup>3</sup>, a position he said held up until Elizondo's resignation, effective 2017-10-04.<sup>4</sup> Sherwood said he reached this conclusion after speaking with OUSDI leadership, including individuals who had been in the office since Elizondo started there.<sup>5</sup> He acknowledged that Elizondo worked for other organisations within the [United States Department of Defense (DoD)](/organisations/united-states-department-of-defense-dod).<sup>6</sup> Separately, Sherwood confirmed that AATIP existed and had pursued research and investigation into unidentified aerial phenomena.<sup>7</sup> He said he could not confirm a statement by Dana White that the programme existed and was run by Elizondo.<sup>8</sup>

The Pentagon's earlier position on Elizondo's AATIP role, which the Harry Reid memo to William Lynn III on Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program (AATIP) Special Access Program status (2009-07-24) was reported not to change, had been issued to The Intercept by Sherwood.<sup>9</sup>

Keith Kloor said that Sherwood's statement directly contradicted an email sent by a spokesperson for [To the Stars Academy of Arts and Science (TTSA)](/organisations/to-the-stars-academy-of-arts-and-science-ttsa).<sup>10</sup> Sherwood denied that Elizondo had any involvement in AATIP, stating on behalf of the Pentagon that "Mr. Elizondo had no responsibilities with regard to the AATIP program."<sup>11</sup> Sherwood privately admitted to Elizondo that he knew of Elizondo's AATIP role, but said forces within the Pentagon had instructed him not to admit it.<sup>12</sup> According to Elizondo, Brad Byers, working in the office of the Secretary of Defense, telephoned Sherwood to express concern over the Pentagon's account, warning that too many senior officials knew of Elizondo's role in AATIP.<sup>13</sup>
