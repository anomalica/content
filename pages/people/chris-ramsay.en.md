Chris Ramsay is a magician and podcaster who hosts the DEBRIEFED interview series, which focuses on UAP, the [alien abduction phenomenon](/topics/alien-abduction-phenomenon), and related anomalous topics. His background in stage illusion and hypnosis informs his analytical approach to witness accounts and photographic evidence. He holds the view that direct testimony from experiencers is the primary source of meaningful information about non-human intelligence, arguing that sky observations, forward-looking infrared (FLIR) footage, and whistleblower accounts cannot substitute for speaking with someone who has had direct contact.<sup>1</sup>

Ramsay has discussed [screen memory](/topics/screen-memory) as a recurring feature of the abduction phenomenon, characterising such memories as conspicuously absurd and abstract, with witnesses sensing that whatever they perceive is somehow misplaced.<sup>2</sup> He draws on his professional knowledge of hypnosis — which he has used in entertainment — to argue that understanding the mechanics of hypnotic suggestion gives a person an advantage in recognising and resisting it, in a manner analogous to seeing through a screen memory.<sup>3</sup> He maintains that, despite reduced media coverage, the [alien abduction phenomenon](/topics/alien-abduction-phenomenon) has not abated, citing accounts from abductees he has spoken with directly.<sup>4</sup> He acknowledges that [sleep paralysis](/topics/sleep-paralysis) symptoms closely resemble abduction experiences and considers it a fair candidate explanation for a subset of cases.<sup>5</sup>

Ramsay has reported personal experience with [out-of-body experience (Out-of-Body Experience (OBE))](/topics/out-of-body-experience-out-of-body-experience-obe), describing episodes that he classifies as distinct from waking states and lucid dreams.<sup>6</sup> Initial involuntary episodes he found terrifying but immediately wished to repeat.<sup>7</sup> He subsequently pursued [out-of-body experience (Out-of-Body Experience (OBE))](/topics/out-of-body-experience-out-of-body-experience-obe) deliberately through the [Gateway Process](/topics/gateway-process), meditating for one hour a day at approximately 04:30 for four months, during which he reports leaving his body many times.<sup>8</sup> In interviews, he has also relayed the account of [Michael Herrera](/people/michael-herrera) concerning the [Michael Herrera Indonesia black disc encounter](/events/michael-herrera-indonesia-black-disc-encounter): a military whistleblower from an elite SEAL team on a humanitarian flood mission who encountered an octagonal black disc hovering over trees,<sup>9</sup> and whose team observed unbadged military personnel loading what appeared to be people, including children, into what Herrera described as reverse-engineered craft.<sup>10</sup> The unbadged personnel, who bore no flags, badges, or names, confiscated the team's phones and instructed them to stay silent.<sup>11</sup>
