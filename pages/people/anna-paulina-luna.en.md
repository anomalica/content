[Tim Burchett](/people/tim-burchett), Matt Gaetz, Anna Paulina Luna and [Eric Burlison](/people/eric-burlison) visited USA, Florida, Eglin Air Force Base to seek answers about [Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP)](/topics/unidentified-anomalous-phenomena-uap).<sup>1</sup> Gaetz had sought a briefing about the incident at Eglin and brought Burchett and Luna with him.<sup>2</sup> Burchett said that without Gaetz's involvement, he, Luna and Burlison would still be at Eglin trying to get answers about UAP.<sup>3</sup>

Luna was one of several members waived onto the House Subcommittee by Glenn Grothman to participate in the House Hearing on Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (2023-07-26), alongside Burchett, Gaetz, Burlison, Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez and Andrew Ogles.<sup>4</sup> According to the hearing's record, Representatives Luna, Burlison, Gaetz, Burchett, James Comer, Ogles, Nick Langworthy, Jamie Raskin and Ocasio-Cortez were present.<sup>5</sup> Grothman thanked Burchett and Luna for bringing UAP to the attention of the [United States House Committee on Oversight and Accountability](/organisations/united-states-house-committee-on-oversight-and-accountability).<sup>6</sup>

During the hearing, Luna described how, while serving as an active-duty service member working on an airfield, she had spoken with pilots who feared coming forward about UAP sightings, worried about retribution or being taken off flight status.<sup>7</sup> Members of [United States Congress](/organisations/united-states-congress) were denied access to a classified briefing in a Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility ahead of the hearing, due to the requirements needed to grant [David Charles Grusch](/people/david-charles-grusch) temporary clearance to testify.<sup>8</sup> Grusch has knowledge of classified information.<sup>9</sup>

Luna also cited polling data during the hearing: she said one survey found that 68 percent of Americans believe the government is hiding information about UAP and is not being honest about what it knows,<sup>10</sup> and another found that nearly half of Americans believe the federal government is doing a very bad or somewhat bad job of dealing with reports of [Unidentified Flying Object (UFO)](/topics/unidentified-flying-object-ufo) sightings.<sup>11</sup> Over 20 percent of Americans have reported having seen UFOs or UAP.<sup>12</sup> Luna said that on 2023-04-19, Sean Kirkpatrick, then head of the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office, stated that he had not found any evidence of unidentified anomalous phenomena.<sup>13</sup>

At the 2024 United States congressional hearing on UAPs (13 November), Luna asked [Luis Elizondo](/people/luis-elizondo) whether it is likely that non-human biologics piloting aircraft are connected via a mind-body connection.<sup>14</sup> Luna has since chaired a congressional hearing on [Project MKUltra (MKUltra)](/projects/project-mkultra-mkultra).<sup>15</sup> As one of the principals of the Congressional Unidentified Aerial Phenomena (UAP) Caucus, Luna has pushed for the disclosure of approximately 46 videos requested from the Defense Department.<sup>16</sup> As of 2026-08-10, it remained unknown whether Luna would gain access to those videos.<sup>17</sup> [Phil McGraw](/people/phil-mcgraw) has discussed the UAP subject with [Donald Trump](/people/donald-trump), Stephen Miller, Burchett and Luna.<sup>18</sup>
