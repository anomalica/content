Eric Burlison is a US Representative for Missouri's 7th congressional district who has become an active participant in congressional efforts to obtain government disclosure on unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAP). He was among the members who travelled to USA, Florida, Eglin Air Force Base alongside [Tim Burchett](/people/tim-burchett), Matt Gaetz, and [Anna Paulina Luna](/people/anna-paulina-luna) to seek answers on UAP.<sup>1</sup> Burchett said that without Gaetz's involvement, the group would still have been at the base trying to get answers.<sup>2</sup>

Burlison was one of several members waived onto the House Oversight Committee's relevant subcommittee by Glenn Grothman to take part in the 2023-07-26 House Hearing on Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena, alongside Burchett, Luna, Gaetz, Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez, and Andrew Ogles.<sup>3</sup> Also present at the hearing were James Comer, Nick Langworthy, and Jamie Raskin.<sup>4</sup>

On 2025-03-27, Burlison announced the appointment of [David Charles Grusch](/people/david-charles-grusch), a former US Air Force officer, as a Special Advisor.<sup>5</sup> According to the announcement, Grusch's expertise would support Burlison's push for transparency on UAP and related matters before the Task Force on the Declassification of Federal Secrets.<sup>6</sup> Burlison said that the American people deserve answers about [Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP)](/topics/unidentified-anomalous-phenomena-uap),<sup>7</sup> that Grusch had risked his career to expose critical information,<sup>8</sup> and that he considered Grusch's expertise vital to uncovering the truth about UAP and holding the government accountable.<sup>9</sup> Grusch, for his part, said he was humbled to join Burlison's team in pushing for government transparency,<sup>10</sup> and that he intended to use his expertise to help the [United States Congress](/organisations/united-states-congress) hold accountable those who lied to Congress, the [Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI)](/organisations/office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence-odni), and the United States Department of Justice (DOJ).<sup>11</sup>

In a 2026-06-30 appearance, Burlison said he had been briefed about an event at a military installation in the Western United States where plasmoid-type phenomena were observed.<sup>12</sup> He said that when he asked ODNI officials what the phenomena were, they told him they did not know, and confirmed their top scientists did not know either.<sup>13</sup> Burlison also distinguished the [Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program (AAWSAP)](/projects/advanced-aerospace-weapon-system-applications-program-aawsap) from what people describe as the "[Legacy Program](/projects/legacy-program)," saying the two are not necessarily the same thing,<sup>14</sup> and said that the gatekeeper structure protecting the legacy program still exists today.<sup>15</sup>

Grusch has described working part-time as a professional staff member for Burlison,<sup>16</sup> and said he attended a classified meeting with Burlison in which he challenged the [All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO)](/organisations/all-domain-anomaly-resolution-office-aaro) of the [United States Department of Defense (DoD)](/organisations/united-states-department-of-defense-dod).<sup>17</sup>
