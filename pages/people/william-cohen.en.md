William Cohen was a United States Senator whose staff included [Christopher K. Mellon](/people/christopher-k-mellon), who worked as a legislative assistant to Cohen before Cohen later became Secretary of Defense.<sup>1</sup> According to Luis Elizondo, during Cohen's time in the Senate, Mellon was his closest advisor on Capitol Hill.<sup>2</sup>

In 1997 Cohen was appointed Secretary of Defense, and Mellon followed him into the United States Pentagon as a member of the new Secretary's transition team.<sup>3</sup>

According to Elizondo, once Cohen took up the post, he brought Mellon onto his staff at the [United States Department of Defense (DoD)](/organisations/united-states-department-of-defense-dod) as one of his trusted principals.<sup>4</sup>
