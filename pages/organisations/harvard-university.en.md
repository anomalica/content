Harvard University's faculty and affiliated researchers have engaged with unidentified anomalous phenomena and adjacent fields across several disciplines, from astrophysics to psychiatry to psychical research. Among the most prominent was [John Mack](/people/john-mack), described as a Harvard researcher<sup>1</sup> and a trained psychiatrist at the university who studied people reporting experiences with the phenomenon.<sup>2</sup> After Mack's death, [Ralph Blumenthal](/people/ralph-blumenthal) spent several years working on his biography.<sup>3</sup>

In 1996, Mack travelled to [Australia Sydney](/places/new-south-wales-sydney-australia) to research the subject of [alien abduction](/topics/alien-abduction).<sup>4</sup> David Charles Grusch has said that Mack concluded something real was occurring in the experiences reported by people who encountered the phenomenon, and that it was not a psychosomatic or mental health condition.<sup>5</sup>

Harvard was also the setting for a 2002-2003 debate that reignited the idea of psychological mechanisms as a possible explanation for alien abduction reports.<sup>6</sup> Susan Clancy and Richard McNally were the key participants in that debate.<sup>7</sup>

Maxwell Frost has said that in 2021 Harvard stood up the Galileo Project to research and examine the origins of [Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP)](/topics/unidentified-anomalous-phenomena-uap).<sup>8</sup> [Luis Elizondo](/people/luis-elizondo) met with Harvard students participating in seminars run by the project, charged with mapping ways to investigate life in the universe.<sup>9</sup>

Harvard's connection to aerospace-adjacent research reaches back further. Shortly before the Second World War, [Vannevar Bush](/people/vannevar-bush) worked closely with [Donald Menzel](/people/donald-menzel), then an astronomer at Harvard, on the development of the differential analyzer, described as the world's first modern analogue computer.<sup>10</sup> Writing in 1950, Bush recalled that Menzel was then an assistant or associate professor in Harvard's astronomy department, interested in applying the machine to astronomical and astrophysical problems.<sup>11</sup> Menzel went on to serve as [Carl Sagan](/people/carl-sagan)'s mentor at Harvard.<sup>12</sup>

Other Harvard-affiliated figures span physics, parapsychology and psychology. Lisa Randall is a theoretical physicist at the university,<sup>13</sup> Alan Gauld spent a postgraduate year there,<sup>14</sup> and Rudy Schild is a retired tenured professor of astrophysics at Harvard.<sup>15</sup> William James of Harvard is credited with "discovering" Leonora E. Piper for psychical research.<sup>16</sup> Christopher Chabris is also affiliated with the university.<sup>17</sup> According to a 2012 conference report on cultural and linguistic capabilities for counterinsurgency strategy, Sahana Dharmapuri was a Fellow at the Carr Center for Human Rights Policy at Harvard.<sup>18</sup>
