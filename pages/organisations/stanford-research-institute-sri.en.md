The Stanford Research Institute (SRI) is a research institute in USA, California, Menlo Park. In response to Soviet spending on psychic phenomena, the [Central Intelligence Agency (CIA)](/organisations/central-intelligence-agency-cia) began covertly funding its own research into psychic phenomena at SRI.<sup>1</sup>

[Ingo Swann](/people/ingo-swann) was approached by SRI scientists to take part in this research.<sup>2</sup> At SRI, Swann developed a distinctive approach to [remote viewing](/topics/remote-viewing-rv) called [Coordinate Remote Viewing](/topics/coordinate-remote-viewing) (CRV).<sup>3</sup> In 1973 he began working with SRI scientists to explore the possibility of remote-viewing planetary objects,<sup>4</sup> an effort that included the Jupiter remote viewing session. This work formed part of a wider programme, later known as the Stanford Research Institute psychic spy program and run for the CIA under the name [Project STARGATE](/projects/project-stargate) from 1972.<sup>5</sup> The programme originated during the Project MKUltra period, prompted by Soviet [extrasensory perception (ESP)](/topics/extrasensory-perception-esp) research.<sup>6</sup>

In 1973, [Pat Price](/people/pat-price), one of the leading psychics in SRI's remote-viewing programme, gave [Harold E. Puthoff](/people/harold-e-puthoff) an unsolicited package of remote-viewing notes describing four alleged "UFO bases" on Earth, an episode recorded as the 1973 Pat Price remote-viewing of alleged "UFO bases" for Hal Puthoff.<sup>7</sup>

[Jacques Vallée](/people/jacques-vallee) said that the SRI experiments with Swann and Price suggest that remote viewing is based on an addressing scheme.<sup>8</sup> According to Vallée's book "The Invisible College", a contact of his observed an unusual object rise from a depression near the Stanford linear accelerator, west of Menlo Park, one evening in February 1972.<sup>9</sup>

[Uri Geller](/people/uri-geller) said that during his first interview with SRI scientists, he told them that the computers of SPECTRA, the alleged cosmic intelligence he attributed his powers to, "were fed millions of light-years in the future", using light-years as a unit of time.<sup>10</sup>

SRI's involvement extended beyond remote viewing. After discussions with Vince Dipietro and Greg Molenaar, [Richard C. Hoagland](/people/richard-c-hoagland) secured funding for the first Independent Mars Investigation under SRI's auspices.<sup>11</sup> More broadly, parapsychologists at institutions such as the Princeton Engineering Anomalies Research (PEAR) Lab and SRI conducted consciousness research studies.<sup>12</sup>
