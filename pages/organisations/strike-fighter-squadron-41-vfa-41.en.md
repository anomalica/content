Strike Fighter Squadron 41 (VFA-41), nicknamed the Black Aces, is a United States Navy F/A-18F squadron.<sup>1</sup> David Fravor, who commanded the squadron, described it as consisting of 12 airplanes and several hundred people.<sup>2</sup>

In November 2004, VFA-41 was attached to Carrier Air Wing 11 (CVW-11) and stationed aboard [USS Nimitz (CVN-68)](/objects/uss-nimitz-cvn-68).<sup>3</sup> The wing also included [Marine Fighter Attack Squadron 232 (VMFA-232)](/organisations/marine-fighter-attack-squadron-232-vmfa-232) flying the F/A-18C, Strike Fighter Squadron 14 (VFA-14) flying the F/A-18E, Strike Fighter Squadron 94 (VFA-94) flying the F/A-18C, Electronic Attack Squadron 135 (VAQ-135) flying the EA-6B, Carrier Airborne Early Warning Squadron 117 (VAW-117) flying the E-2C Hawkeye, Helicopter Antisubmarine Squadron 6 (HS-6) flying the H-60, and Fleet Logistics Support Squadron 30 Detachment 3 (VRC-30 Det 3) flying the C-2A, according to the Nimitz Carrier Strike Group incident report.<sup>4</sup>

On 2004-11-14, F/A-18 pilots and weapon system operators from VFA-41 obtained both visual and sensor contact with the [Anomalous Aerial Vehicle (AAV)](/topics/anomalous-aerial-vehicle-aav) south of the U.S./Mexico border, off the Baja California coast, according to the incident report.<sup>1</sup> The squadron's commanding officer, [David Fravor](/people/david-fravor), piloted the lead F/A-18F, call sign FastEagle 01, one of two VFA-41 aircraft involved in the intercept and visual contact.<sup>5</sup> [Alex Dietrich](/people/alex-dietrich) piloted the second aircraft, FastEagle 02.<sup>6</sup> A second VFA-41 section was also airborne that day, with [Chad Underwood](/people/chad-underwood) serving as weapon system operator alongside another lieutenant.<sup>7, 8</sup> The incident report states that the only units involved in the detection and intercept of the AAV were [USS Princeton (CG-59)](/objects/uss-princeton-cg-59), VAW-117, VMFA-232, and VFA-41, and that all AAV activity was reported to D.C. Curtis by the commanding officers of USS Princeton and VFA-41.<sup>9, 4</sup>

Fravor commanded VFA-41 during this period and flew the two-seat variant of the F/A-18F Super Hornet in that role.<sup>10, 11</sup> Earlier in his career, during a freshman cruise aboard USS Nimitz, Fravor had observed Fighter Squadron 41 (VF-41) and Fighter Squadron 84 (VF-84), an F-14 Tomcat squadron, together with A-6 Intruder squadrons operating from the carrier.<sup>12</sup>
