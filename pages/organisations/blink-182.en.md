Blink-182 is an American rock band. Its front man was [Tom DeLonge](/people/tom-delonge).<sup>1</sup> By 1999, as the band's lead singer, DeLonge had become a megastar, packing stadiums full of fans across the United States and around the world.<sup>2</sup>

In June 1999 Blink-182 released their album Enema of the State, whose cover featured pornographic actress Janine Lindemulder in a nurse's uniform.<sup>3</sup> John Lydon, the former Sex Pistols singer known as Johnny Rotten, said that he dismissed Blink-182 as "a bunch of silly boys."<sup>4</sup>

DeLonge developed an interest in the [UFO](/topics/unidentified-flying-object-ufo) subject from a very young age, with footage of him discussing it possibly predating the band's formation.<sup>5</sup> Jason Samosa said that DeLonge stated on podcasts that he had met the people managing the UFO subject and that they had begun speaking to him.<sup>6</sup>

DeLonge quit Blink-182 in 2015,<sup>7</sup> a move that put the band on indefinite hiatus and was driven in part by his wish to focus on researching UAP.<sup>8</sup> This departure is recorded as the 2015 Tom DeLonge departure from Blink-182. Shortly before leaving, DeLonge was invited to an open house for families of a top defence aerospace contracting company, where he served as master of ceremonies and introduced a lead aerospace executive.<sup>9</sup>
