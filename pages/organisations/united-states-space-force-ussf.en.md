The United States Space Force (USSF) is the sixth and newest branch of the US military, established in December 2019.<sup>1</sup> This coincides with the creation of the United States Space Force as a distinct service. Its Latin motto is "Si Vis Pacem, Para Bellum", meaning "If you want peace, prepare for war".<sup>2</sup> According to *In Plain Sight*, the USSF's stated vision includes developing a world-class team of space professionals building combat-ready space forces and space warfighting capabilities.<sup>3</sup>

Christopher K. Mellon said that the [United States Air Force (USAF)](/organisations/united-states-air-force-usaf) oversees the Space Force and has far more aircraft and sensor systems than the [United States Navy (USN)](/organisations/united-states-navy-usn), as well as responsibility for supporting [North American Aerospace Defense Command (NORAD)](/organisations/north-american-aerospace-defense-command-norad).<sup>4</sup>

The USSF's connection to [Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP)](/topics/unidentified-anomalous-phenomena-uap) work runs through [Luis Elizondo](/people/luis-elizondo). Elizondo said that the USSF did not yet have a UAP programme when [Jay Stratton](/people/jay-stratton) needed a representative from the organisation for the task force he was building, understood to be the [Unidentified Aerial Phenomena Task Force (UAPTF)](/projects/unidentified-aerial-phenomena-task-force-uaptf).<sup>5</sup> Elizondo said he then began working as a contractor for the USSF to help build its UAP effort and support Stratton's task force.<sup>6</sup> According to Elizondo, [David Charles Grusch](/people/david-charles-grusch) worked with him at the USSF.<sup>7</sup>

A [Pentagon](/organisations/the-pentagon) spokesperson confirmed to the media, according to Elizondo, that Elizondo provides technical advice on a variety of classified topics for the USSF.<sup>8</sup>
