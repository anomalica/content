Stanford University, in USA, California, Stanford, is a research university whose faculty and researchers have intersected with several strands of UAP-related and fringe research since the mid-20th century. In 1954, three Stanford sociologists infiltrated a small UFO group led by Marian Keech, who had predicted the arrival of UFOs amid a global cataclysm.<sup>1</sup> Contrary to the sociologists' expectation that Keech's followers would come to disbelieve her, some followers instead reinterpreted the events in a way that supported their original faith in her.<sup>2</sup>

Stanford was also a centre of early [remote viewing (RV)](/topics/remote-viewing-rv) research. [Harold E. Puthoff](/people/harold-e-puthoff) earned his PhD from Stanford University in 1967.<sup>3</sup> The technique later used in [Project Stargate](/projects/stargate) was pioneered at Stanford in the late 1960s, led by Puthoff.<sup>4</sup> He was a Stanford researcher and an employee of the [National Security Agency (NSA)](/organisations/national-security-agency-nsa) when he and [Russell Targ](/people/russell-targ) were approached by the [Central Intelligence Agency (CIA)](/organisations/central-intelligence-agency-cia) and told that Russia had a remote viewing programme.<sup>5</sup> Puthoff's broader career spanned research at General Electric, the Sperry Corporation, the NSA, Stanford, and [SRI International](/organisations/sri-international).<sup>6</sup>

According to the Report on the Historical Record of U.S. Government Involvement with Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP), Volume I, Stanford's Sturrock Panel (1998) found no convincing evidence for the extraterrestrial origin of UFO/UAP.<sup>7</sup>

Elisabeth Targ spent a decade at Stanford, becoming a certified Russian translator before earning her medical degree there.<sup>8</sup> By 1982, Florenze Mansmann held a doctorate in biomedical engineering and worked at Stanford.<sup>9</sup>

[Garry Nolan](/people/garry-nolan) is a Professor in the Department of Pathology at Stanford University.<sup>10</sup> He held a chaired position and his own laboratory there, where postdoctoral researchers and graduate students investigated human genetics.<sup>11</sup> William "Will" Livingston's team brought MRI images of the brains of UAP experiencers, all servicemen and intelligence officials, to Nolan's office at Stanford.<sup>12</sup> Nolan was a friend and colleague of [Luis Elizondo](/people/luis-elizondo) and was included on [Tom DeLonge](/people/tom-delonge)'s advisory board.<sup>13</sup> Nolan is also a professor at Stanford's medical school, and the Nolan/Vallée Iowa Unidentified Aerial Phenomena (UAP) materials paper was the first time he had published in an aerospace journal.<sup>14</sup>

According to In Plain Sight, a Stanford analysis and a 2020 analysis by Robert Powell of the Scientific Coalition for Unidentified Aerial Phenomena (UAP) Studies, published as the 2020 Robert Powell analysis of the Ubatuba samples for the Scientific Coalition for Unidentified Aerial Phenomena (UAP) Studies, suggested there is no evidence that the Ubatuba object samples are of extra-terrestrial origin, nor that the magnesium in them is especially pure.<sup>15</sup>

[David Fravor](/people/david-fravor) said that [Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)](/organisations/massachusetts-institute-of-technology-mit), [California Institute of Technology (Caltech)](/organisations/california-institute-of-technology-caltech), Stanford University, Georgia Institute of Technology (Georgia Tech), Virginia Polytechnic Institute and State University, Carnegie Mellon University, and the Naval Postgraduate School are among the top-tier universities from which leading-edge technology research typically emerges.<sup>16</sup>

Philip Zimbardo conducted the Stanford Prison Experiment at Stanford University.<sup>17</sup>
