Barry Goldwater served on the [Senate Armed Services Committee](/organisations/senate-armed-services-committee) and the Aeronautical and Space Sciences Committee, and was a former chairman of the Senate Intelligence Committee.<sup>1</sup> [Harry Reid](/people/harry-reid) served for two years on the committee.<sup>2</sup>

[Christopher K. Mellon](/people/christopher-k-mellon) worked as a staffer on the committee in [D.C. Washington](/places/district-of-columbia-washington-usa)<sup>3</sup> and later served as its Minority Staff Director.<sup>4</sup> In that capacity he reviewed top-secret [Sensitive Compartmented Information](/topics/sensitive-compartmented-information-facility-scif) projects held within [Special Access Programs](/topics/special-access-program-sap), most of which remain classified,<sup>5</sup> and monitored [Area 51](/places/nevada-area-51-usa), a highly secure [United States Air Force (USAF)](/organisations/united-states-air-force-usaf) facility within the Nevada test range, on the committee's behalf.<sup>6</sup>

According to "Intelligence Officials Say U.S. Has Retrieved Craft of Non-Human Origin", the first briefing Mellon facilitated on retrievals of unexplained objects was given to staff of the Senate Armed Services Committee on 2019-10-21, with a briefing to Senate Intelligence Committee staff following two days later.<sup>7</sup> [Eric Davis](/people/eric-davis) briefed [United States Department of Defense (DoD)](/organisations/united-states-department-of-defense-dod) agencies and staff of both the Senate Armed Services Committee and the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence on the government's possession of off-world vehicles.<sup>8</sup> The congressional strategy pursued by Mellon and his colleagues began by engaging key professional staff of the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence and the Senate Armed Services Committee, as well as members of [United States Congress](/organisations/united-states-congress).<sup>9</sup>

In late 2020 the committee demanded that US intelligence agencies and the Department of Defense compile a report on all data collected on [Unidentified Aerial Phenomena (UAP)](/topics/unidentified-anomalous-phenomena-uap), including navy pilot sightings, in what became known as the late 2020 US Senate Intelligence Committee demand for a UAP report.<sup>10</sup> In December 2020, while serving as head of the committee, [Marco Rubio](/people/marco-rubio) asked the [Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI)](/organisations/office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence-odni) and the Pentagon to present Congress with an unclassified report on UAP within a month,<sup>11</sup> and the committee ordered the Director of National Intelligence and the Secretary of Defense to deliver a report within the same timeframe.<sup>12</sup> The report was due in June 2021 but was likely to be delayed.<sup>13</sup> According to the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence Report on the FY 2021 Intelligence Authorization Act, dated 2020-06-08, the US government did not have a unified, comprehensive process in place for collecting and analysing intelligence on UAP.<sup>14</sup>

Mark Warner, the former chairman of the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence, and his replacement, Marco Rubio, strongly and publicly supported UAP legislation in the [National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2023](/documents/national-defense-authorization-act-for-fiscal-year-2023-fy2023-national-defense-authorization-act-ndaa).<sup>15</sup>

[David Charles Grusch](/people/david-charles-grusch)'s whistleblower complaint brought the committee into a further UAP-related controversy. A summary of his complaint was submitted to the Director of National Intelligence, Avril Haines, the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence, and the House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence.<sup>16</sup> In the summer of 2022, the [Inspector General of the Intelligence Community](/organisations/inspector-general-of-the-intelligence-community) was required to forward Grusch's complaint to both committees,<sup>17</sup> and Grusch subsequently gave 11 and a half hours of classified testimony to the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence and the House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence.<sup>18</sup> Grusch has said that the two committees opened a full-field investigation after his testimony, but that there was some fear within them, especially in the Senate.<sup>19</sup>

Not everyone claiming knowledge of nonhuman craft has been willing to approach the committee. According to "The Fourth Mind", the anonymous author of a Reddit document on nonhuman anatomy, when asked whether he had shared his account with the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence or the [All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO)](/organisations/all-domain-anomaly-resolution-office-aaro), refused, saying it sounded like a honey trap, that he would not place his life in the hands of politicians, and that he had no proof other than the message itself.<sup>20</sup>
