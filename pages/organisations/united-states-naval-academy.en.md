Before enrolling at the United States Naval Academy, [David Fravor](/people/david-fravor) served as an enlisted member of the [United States Marine Corps (USMC)](/organisations/united-states-marine-corps-usmc).<sup>1</sup> The Marine Corps recommended him for admission to the Academy.<sup>2</sup> Fravor subsequently attended and graduated from the United States Naval Academy.<sup>3</sup>

During his time at the Academy, Fravor undertook his freshman midshipman cruise aboard the [USS Nimitz (CVN-68)](/objects/uss-nimitz-cvn-68), following his first year of study there.<sup>4</sup>

Fravor has stated that it took exactly two years from the day he graduated from the Naval Academy until he received his naval aviator wings.<sup>5</sup>
