On 24 April 1964, according to *In Plain Sight*, police officer [Lonnie Zamora](/people/lonnie-zamora) witnessed a flaming object in the sky near [New Mexico Socorro](/places/new-mexico-socorro-usa) and went to investigate.<sup>1</sup> Zamora was the main witness at the resulting landing site.<sup>2</sup> Arriving at the scene, according to the same source, he came across a shiny whitish aluminium-coloured craft landed in a dry creek bed, with two small figures standing in white coveralls alongside it.<sup>3</sup> This is corroborated in substance by *Imminent: Inside the Pentagon's Hunt for UFOs*, in which Zamora is described as having observed two figures dressed in white uniforms near the object.<sup>4</sup>

As Zamora approached, the two figures reportedly saw him and dashed aboard the object.<sup>5</sup> According to *In Plain Sight*, the oval-shaped object then rose off the ground, and Zamora noticed red lettering on its side.<sup>6</sup> A separate account in *Imminent* describes Zamora as having seen an egg-shaped object bearing markings or insignia on its side.<sup>7</sup> One detail of these markings received particular attention in later research: Jacques Vallée wrote that a friend of his at Stanford, shown a description of the symbol, found a medieval Arabic text listing symbols for the major planets that unmistakably matched the insignia Zamora saw, identifying it as the Arabic sign for Venus.<sup>8</sup>

Accounts of the object's departure describe it as having no visible means of propulsion, rising from the ground with a roar, spewing blue-orange flames and leaving scorched vegetation behind.<sup>9</sup> A related passage in *Imminent* states that the craft took off from the desert floor with a loud roar and a blast of blue flame.<sup>10</sup> Zamora is reported to have run up a ravine to escape, fearing the craft might explode.<sup>11</sup> After reaching a certain height above the ground, the craft is said to have flown away silently.<sup>12</sup>

Physical traces were documented at the site afterwards. Indentations and charred bushes were found on the ground where the craft had landed.<sup>13</sup> Independent witnesses reportedly confirmed seeing an egg-shaped craft or bluish flame near Socorro at about the same time as Zamora's sighting.<sup>14</sup> Other witnesses in the region are said to have reported an egg-shaped object streaking across the landscape before or after the encounter,<sup>15</sup> and a further account describes a witness nearby reporting that a white, butane-tank-shaped object landed on his family's property at night, frightening the family's horses, before streaking away.<sup>16</sup> Separately, Raymond E. Fowler recounted that [William T. Powers](/people/william-t-powers), an Air Force astronomer, helped investigate the landing.<sup>17</sup> Fowler further stated that the object was seen or heard by about 13 different people,<sup>18</sup> and that physical evidence found afterwards included ground indentations, scorched areas, still-burning greasewood, and a melted, calcined bottle.<sup>19</sup> Investigators from Kirtland Air Force Base measured radioactivity at the site where a bush had been burned.<sup>20</sup>

The encounter drew attention from multiple government bodies. The [United States Air Force (USAF)](/organisations/united-states-air-force-usaf) investigated the incident,<sup>21</sup> as did the [United States Army](/organisations/united-states-army)<sup>22</sup> and the [Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI)](/organisations/federal-bureau-of-investigation-fbi).<sup>23</sup> [J. Allen Hynek](/people/j-allen-hynek) visited Socorro to investigate the case.<sup>24</sup> According to *Imminent*, the Air Force warned Zamora not to share knowledge of the markings he had seen with civilians.<sup>25</sup>

In the days that followed, the story appeared in newspapers and on radio and television broadcasts throughout the United States.<sup>26</sup> Despite the publicity, Zamora is reported to have never tried to profit from the encounter and did not seek out media attention.<sup>27</sup>
