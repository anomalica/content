The 1980 Rendlesham Forest incident is a series of unidentified aerial phenomena (UAP) sightings reported by United States Air Force personnel in and around [Rendlesham Forest](/places/england-rendlesham-forest-uk), Suffolk, England, adjacent to the joint US-UK air bases of RAF Woodbridge and RAF Bentwaters, over several nights in December 1980. An American military patrol near RAF Woodbridge saw lights descending into the forest.<sup>1</sup> Security policemen [John Burroughs](/people/john-burroughs) and [Jim Penniston](/people/jim-penniston) reported finding a landed UAP in the forest, near a facility where the United States and the United Kingdom had stored nuclear weapons in a secret bunker.<sup>2</sup> That same month, a UAP was reported to have hovered over the underground bunker itself.<sup>3</sup> According to In Plain Sight, Penniston and Burroughs said they saw a craft land near the base on 1980-12-26.<sup>4</sup>

Penniston described a small, three-metre-wide craft made of what looked like a black, smoky glass-like material, with unfamiliar symbols inscribed on its upper surface.<sup>5</sup> According to Imminent: Inside the Pentagon's Hunt for UFOs, witnesses said the symbols resembled those reported at other UAP encounters.<sup>6</sup> Penniston later said that he had touched the craft and recorded sketches of the markings in a notebook.<sup>7</sup> He also drew a sketch of the object.<sup>8</sup> He said that he and his colleagues experienced what he described as "missing time" during the encounter, with their memories deliberately confused and blocked in some way.<sup>9</sup> The watches worn by Burroughs and Penniston were found to have lost forty-five minutes compared with the timepieces of airmen who had remained on base.<sup>10</sup> Luis Elizondo said that [Harold Puthoff](/people/harold-e-puthoff) calls this phenomenon of missing or lost time, as reported in relation to the incident, the Rip Van Winkle Effect.<sup>11</sup> Burroughs, for his part, described strange flashing red, green and blue lights in a forest clearing that threw off the image of a craft, but said he never saw anything metallic or hard.<sup>12</sup>

Two nights after the initial sighting, [United States Air Force (USAF)](/organisations/united-states-air-force-usaf) Colonel Charles Halt, deputy commander of the nearby RAF Bentwaters base, led a patrol back into the forest following another reported landing, and later wrote a detailed memorandum of what he saw.<sup>13</sup> Halt said the patrol witnessed an object he described as "intelligently-controlled" moving through the forest.<sup>14</sup> He made an audiotape of his live commentary during the encounter, describing the object as looking "like an eye winking at you".<sup>15</sup> Halt and his patrol watched the object, which had "danced about in the forest", shed sparks that looked like molten metal, amid a heavy field of static electricity in the air.<sup>16</sup> He said it shone beams of light into the nuclear weapons storage area on the RAF Bentwaters base.<sup>17</sup> Halt has said he witnessed the object hovering at close range and believed it was probably extra-terrestrial.<sup>18</sup> He has also said he is adamant that the objects he saw were intelligently controlled and probably non-human in origin,<sup>19</sup> and that he believes something was controlling what he saw, describing the experience as "a bit scary".<sup>20</sup>

Halt has said the nearby lighthouse was easily visible and quite distinct, at least 30 degrees away from what he saw during the encounter.<sup>21</sup> Ian Ridpath and other sceptics have said that all the men saw was the lighthouse, a misperception of distant lights.<sup>22</sup> Nick Pope has said that radiation levels tested the day after the incident were many times higher than normal background, though this finding was challenged by sceptics.<sup>23</sup> According to In Plain Sight, radar operators have since testified that an unknown object was tracked in the area on the night of the incident.<sup>24</sup>

The [Air Force Office of Special Investigations (AFOSI)](/organisations/air-force-office-of-special-investigations-afosi) attacked the credibility of Halt's sighting.<sup>25</sup> Halt has said he believes there was a cover-up regarding the incident.<sup>26</sup> According to In Plain Sight, Margaret Thatcher was reportedly quoted privately as saying "You can't tell the people" regarding claims of an aggressive official cover-up - the same phrase that titles the account You Can't Tell the People: The Definitive Account of the Rendlesham Forest Unidentified Flying Object (UFO) Mystery.<sup>27</sup> According to Imminent, the eyewitness servicemen were called to the base's AFOSI office the day after the incident,<sup>28</sup> where, according to servicemen the author said he had spoken to, a representative of the [Central Intelligence Agency (CIA)](/organisations/central-intelligence-agency-cia) was present in the room.<sup>29</sup> Years later, according to Imminent, one serviceman said he recognised, from a photograph, a CIA official who had long worked on the UAP topic, and said that official had been in the room the day after the event.<sup>30</sup> Some of the servicemen said they were told never to speak about what they had seen and were administered a drug and hypnotised, presumably to distort their memories, according to Imminent.<sup>31</sup> According to the same account, all communications on the base went to flash override protocol during the incident, a protocol that shuts down communications lines so that only the president of the United States can communicate directly with someone on the base.<sup>32</sup> Servicemen on the base also reported that an unidentified private plane landed on a runway outside the base afterward, and that a group of men said to be from a defence contractor exited the plane and were driven onto the base,<sup>33</sup> before later being seen loading crates onto the plane and departing for the United States.<sup>34</sup> According to Imminent, multiple servicemen who saw the UAP up close have since gone public despite being told not to talk about it.<sup>35</sup> Senator [John McCain](/people/john-mccain) successfully had the service records of one of the servicemen involved declassified,<sup>36</sup> and two of the witnesses were awarded permanent disability by the US government for their involvement and injury.<sup>37</sup> Burroughs subsequently experienced worsening health effects, including vision problems, white gums, heart murmurs and heart tissue scarring, culminating in surgery to correct damaged leaflets in his mitral valve.<sup>38</sup>

A separate early account, attributed to researcher Raymond E. Fowler, describes events on the first night in different detail. Fowler said that a document he obtained described a triangular-shaped object landing at the back gate of RAF Woodbridge, a joint United States Air Force/Royal Air Force base near Ipswich, England.<sup>39</sup> The same document, according to Fowler, described armed security police advancing toward the object before it took off into the woods, lighting the area with blue lights underneath and a red light on top.<sup>40</sup> Fowler said the document described wild animals, including rabbits, running out of the woods after the object took off,<sup>41</sup> and that investigators at the site found depressions in the ground where three landing gear had rested,<sup>42</sup> as well as radiation at the landing site.<sup>43</sup>
