The B-2 Spirit is a [United States Air Force (USAF)](/organisations/united-states-air-force-usaf) low-observable stealth bomber capable of delivering both conventional and nuclear payloads.<sup>1</sup> Its reduced radar visibility is achieved through a combination of reduced infrared, acoustic, and electromagnetic signatures.<sup>2</sup>

The aircraft's design traces back to earlier US stealth aircraft programmes. The HAVE Blue program, the F-117 Nighthawk, and TACIT Blue laid the technical foundations that led to the later development of the B-2 Spirit.<sup>3</sup>

The B-2 Spirit was first publicly displayed on 1988-11-22 in California Palmdale.<sup>4</sup> It conducted its first flight on 1989-07-17,<sup>5</sup> and the first aircraft was delivered to the USAF on 1993-12-17.<sup>6</sup>

A single B-2 Spirit costs over one billion United States dollars.<sup>7</sup>
