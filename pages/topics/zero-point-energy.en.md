Zero-point energy is a concept in quantum physics referring to the residual energy that remains present in a system, including the vacuum of space, even at its lowest possible energy state. According to the source record for one discussion of the topic, it is theorised by some physicists as a powerful energy source existing in the vacuum of space.<sup>1</sup> Within UAP research, it has been repeatedly proposed as a candidate explanation for the propulsion characteristics attributed to unidentified anomalous phenomena, on the reasoning that a genuinely limitless energy source in the vacuum itself could account for manoeuvres otherwise regarded as physically implausible.

The topic has a long history of public discussion by prominent figures. In a 2001 exchange, [Buzz Aldrin](/people/buzz-aldrin) raised the subject of zero-point energy rather than commenting on [Arthur C. Clarke](/people/arthur-c-clarke)'s claim of photographic evidence of life on Mars.<sup>2</sup> Aldrin said he believed it may take 200 to 400 years to reach Alpha Centauri, but that zero-point energy would be needed to do so.<sup>3</sup> Clarke said he was glad Aldrin had raised what he called the controversial question of zero-point energy,<sup>4</sup> while also noting that many of his scientist friends thought it was crazy to believe there was anything to the idea.<sup>5</sup>

Physicist [Harold E. Puthoff](/people/harold-e-puthoff) has been described as an expert on the theory of zero-point energy, positing that free energy exists in the vacuum of space and could in principle be harnessed.<sup>6</sup> Puthoff himself proposed that the energy required to warp space-time might originate from vacuum fluctuations, the underlying quantum fluctuations of empty space, based on the phenomenon of zero-point energy.<sup>7</sup> Gordon Novel's book quoted a source identified as "Wally Katz" as reluctantly admitting that the Fluxliner alien reproduction vehicle was a reproduction of a universal UFO drive design, and that he was willing to fund Puthoff's zero-point energy research and development programme and secretly show how to do it, though the [Anti-Gravity Propulsion](/topics/anti-gravity-propulsion) system involved had to remain what the source called a "Supreme Cosmic Secret" and the number one national security issue.<sup>8</sup> Separately, author Whitley Strieber has said he considers zero-point energy the one known force that might enable the limitless propulsion observed in small [Unidentified Flying Objects](/topics/unidentified-flying-object-ufo).<sup>9</sup>

Others have connected zero-point energy to broader theoretical frameworks. [Richard C. Hoagland](/people/richard-c-hoagland) said he regards the Quantum Electrodynamics Zero Point Energy of space, discussed among Western physicists, as [James Clerk Maxwell](/people/james-clerk-maxwell)'s hyper-dimensional physics theory under another name.<sup>10</sup> According to the source record, current quantum mechanics models hold that the vacuum possesses a staggering amount of zero-point energy per cubic inch of empty space.<sup>11</sup> Paul Murad cited a comment that the torsion field is identical to the transverse spin polarisation of the physical vacuum, and that a gravitational field is identical to its longitudinal spin polarisation.<sup>12</sup> [Chris Ramsay](/people/chris-ramsay) has equated the oneness measured in quantum physics, observed by physicists looking at the quantum vacuum or zero-point field, with the feeling of oneness experienced through unconditional love, a framing that touches on concepts related to quantum entanglement.<sup>13</sup>

[Tom DeLonge](/people/tom-delonge) has made public claims that the US government already knew the secrets of free energy, so-called zero-point energy,<sup>14</sup> and said that one inch of air could power the United States for hundreds of years.<sup>15</sup> When Joe Rogan pressed DeLonge on who exactly was telling him these secrets, DeLonge replied that Rogan did not know what he knew and that he could not tell him some of what he knew.<sup>16</sup> Journalist [Ross Coulthart](/people/ross-coulthart) has described zero-point energy as energy drawn from the quantum vacuum, offering the capacity to draw unlimited amounts of energy through an understanding of quantum science not yet fully explained in the public domain.<sup>17</sup> Coulthart has also said he considers that the development of zero-point energy would be an ontological shock to humanity and particularly to the markets, and would probably wipe out every top 100 company on the American stock exchange overnight,<sup>18</sup> and that he considers many wealthy business people do not want the public to have free energy.<sup>19</sup>
