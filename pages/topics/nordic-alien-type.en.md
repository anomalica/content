Nordic aliens are typically described as tall, blonde and blue-eyed beings said to live discreetly among humans.<sup>1</sup> Tall Nordic-type beings with hair have been reported frequently in abduction cases, most notably the 1975 Travis Walton abduction case involving Travis Walton.<sup>2</sup> Nordic-type beings also appear in the accounts of [Peter Khoury](/people/peter-khoury), including the Peter Khoury alien abduction case of 1992.<sup>3</sup> Within the broader taxonomy of reported alien types, Nordics are considered most conspicuous in contactee accounts describing largely benevolent interactions with humanlike beings.<sup>4</sup>

[Bill Chalker](/people/bill-chalker) said the account given by Vicki Klein shares several features identical to those heralded as a breakthrough for understanding the alien abduction agenda by [Budd Hopkins](/people/budd-hopkins) in 1987, including Nordic-type humanoids involved in a genetic programme using painful ova removal, a room housing bred babies, and weak babies responding to human bonding.<sup>5</sup> According to Hair of the Alien: DNA and Other Forensic Evidence for Alien Abductions, the 1970 Imjarvi, Finland close encounter of Aarno Heinonen and Esko Vijo featured a Nordic female entity carrying a hand-held "ball" artefact, with suggestions of controlling implants.<sup>6</sup> The same source describes the 1968 Villa Carlos Paz, Argentina Nordic entity encounter with Maria Elodia Pretzel, in which a Nordic entity used a ball to manipulate what is described as a solid light phenomenon (UFO) at Argentina Villa Carlos Paz, confronting witness Maria Elodia Pretzel and causing her to slowly fall backwards and rise again at least twice.<sup>7</sup>

Credo Mutwa described beings he termed Wazungu (African term for white-skinned aliens) as tall, athletically built, with slightly slanting blue eyes, high cheekbones, golden hair, and long, musician-like fingers.<sup>8</sup> Bill Chalker said he considered Mutwa's Wazungu account to be describing the Nordic-type beings extensively reported in UFO literature.<sup>9</sup> Richard Thompson said that parallels could be drawn between the Vedic female Devas (goddesses) and UFO entities, particularly Nordic-type aliens.<sup>10</sup> Bill Chalker said he considered the Tuatha de Danann tradition and fairy folklore to feature a similar pairing of figures: tall, often long-haired, blue-eyed beings with striking abilities and resources - the Tuatha and the Nordics - alongside diminutive beings often intent on entrapment and abduction - the fairies and the gray alien stereotype.<sup>11</sup> Bill Chalker said he did not suggest the beings recovered in the 1994 discovery of Xinjiang Taklimakan mummies were related to the Nordic-type beings often reported in abduction stories, considering such a possibility assessable only once a complete DNA profile of the mummies and consistent DNA profiles from a significant number of abduction cases became available.<sup>12</sup>

According to Hair of the Alien, some accounts describe two disparate entity types said to claim they are one people: a tall, blonde figure resembling the Nordic race, and a second, dog-faced type with pointed ears, eyes lacking iris or white, a longish flattened nose and a thin mouth, dressed in a blue-grey tunic with a tight banded collar and oval shoes.<sup>13</sup> Alvin Guerra said he was rewarded for his cooperation with a sexual encounter with the tall, blue-eyed, blond-haired Nordic commander of the UFO.<sup>14</sup>

Ross Coulthart has said that Jason Reza Jorjani, during the 2025-11 Jason Reza Jorjani interview on American Alchemy, claimed the [Central Intelligence Agency (CIA)](/organisations/central-intelligence-agency-cia)'s alleged access to consumer DNA databases is used to screen for genetic markers of Nordic alien beings said to live on Earth.<sup>15</sup> Coulthart has also said that Nordic aliens are said to favour living in the Rocky Mountains of Colorado.<sup>16</sup>
