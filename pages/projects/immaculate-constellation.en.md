Immaculate Constellation is the name of an alleged top-secret program for handling classified US government material on [Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP)](/topics/ufos-uaps), whose existence was reported publicly for the first time by journalist [Michael Shellenberger](/people/michael-shellenberger) in an in-depth report he published on 2024-10-09 on his platform Public.<sup>1</sup> Speaking about the story afterwards, Chris Ramsay described it as a top-secret parent program that had recently been divulged in the news.<sup>2</sup> Shellenberger said he was warned that revealing the name would probably result in him being placed under surveillance,<sup>3</sup> and said he had the name confirmed by a second source.<sup>4</sup>

According to Shellenberger, his report concerns a new whistleblower, either a government employee or a government contractor, who wrote a report and provided it to members of the [United States Congress](/organisations/united-states-congress).<sup>5</sup> Shellenberger said the report claims the [United States Department of Defense (DoD)](/organisations/united-states-department-of-defense-dod) is illegally withholding information from Congress about a secret UAP program called Immaculate Constellation, which the whistleblower describes as a parent program of other, subordinate programs.<sup>6</sup> [Chris Ramsay](/people/chris-ramsay), discussing the report, said it states that Immaculate Constellation handles classified materials related to UAP encounters<sup>7</sup> and reportedly operates under an unacknowledged special access program (USAP).<sup>8</sup> Ramsay also said the program was reportedly initiated in 2017, following the DoD's admission of the [Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program (AATIP)](/projects/advanced-aerospace-threat-identification-program-aatip).<sup>9</sup> Ramsay noted he had not himself been shown the underlying whistleblower report that Shellenberger's piece is based on.<sup>10</sup>

Per Ramsay's account of the report, Immaculate Constellation reportedly tracks, retrieves and archives data on UFO sightings and encounters,<sup>11</sup> including a case involving orbs swarming an F-22 Raptor.<sup>12</sup> Ramsay said the material reportedly includes imagery such as forward-looking infrared (FLIR) footage, full-motion video (FMV), and other surveillance footage documenting UAP encounters.<sup>13</sup> Separately, [David Grush](/people/david-grush) never mentioned Immaculate Constellation, according to Ramsay.<sup>14</sup>

At the 2024 United States congressional hearing on UAPs (13 November), members of Congress reportedly received the Immaculate Constellation document only minutes beforehand, leaving them no time to review it.<sup>15</sup> The document does not contain any content about hybridisation.<sup>16</sup>

In a later discussion, Immaculate Constellation was described as an activity of the [National Security Council](/organisations/national-security-council), established in 2017, said to hold a dataset connected to Avi Loeb's council.<sup>17</sup> Avi Loeb himself said Immaculate Constellation was mentioned in Congress.<sup>18</sup>
